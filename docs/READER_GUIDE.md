# Reader Guide

Northbound helps you take part in your community's reading Challenges: register,
read, submit completed books and follow your progress. Your Northbound reading
history comes from Challenge participation and submissions, not a general lifetime
reading log or an import from another service.

## Start with your account and Group

Open your username menu for **Home**, **My Account** and **My Stats**.

In **My Account**, check your name and recovery email, optionally choose a
**Built-In Avatar** or **Upload Your Own**, and select **Save Account**. Your email
is private and is not displayed in Group rosters. **Change Password** is available
from the same page.

**Discord Username** is optional profile information. Appropriate Group and
Challenge staff can use it for planning. **Make Discord username public** is off
by default; turn it on only if you want other Group members to see it. Entering
a Discord username during registration does not change that preference.

On Home, **Your Reading Groups** shows your communities. If you have an invitation
code, choose **Join With Code**, enter it and **Save**. Joining a Group does not
automatically register you for its Challenges or put you on a team.

## Find a Challenge

Open your Group, then a visible Challenge. A Challenge is a particular reading
event with its own dates, registration, optional activities and scoring rules.
Check **Challenge Schedule**, the registration status and any announcement.

- **Upcoming:** you can register if registration is Open. Book submission has not
  started yet.
- **Active:** registered, active participants can submit completed books.
  Registration may still be Open or may already be Closed.
- **Finalizing:** new submissions and new registrations have ended; outstanding
  reviews can still change results.
- **Completed:** the Challenge is finished and kept for reference.
- **Archived:** the event has been put away rather than erased. Your recorded
  participation remains in My Stats.

Draft Challenges are not normally visible to Readers. **Open** registration is a
separate status, not a lifecycle stage called “Open Registration.” An Upcoming
Challenge is not necessarily accepting registrations yet.

## Register for the Challenge

1. Open the Challenge and choose **Register** when available.
2. Answer its registration questions. Complete required fields; optional questions
   can be left blank. You may be asked for **Discord Username (Optional)** if your
   profile does not already have one.
3. If Personal TBR is enabled, add any books you want to register before continuing.
4. Choose **Review Registration**.
5. Check **Registration Summary**, including your answers and any TBR warnings.
   Use **Back to Edit** if needed.
6. Choose **Confirm Registration** to finish.

You still confirm registration when the Challenge has no custom questions. Team
assignment is separate; a Host will organize it. You do not need a team assignment
just to submit once your participation is active and the Challenge is Active.

### Who can see registration answers?

Your answers are for Challenge planning, not a public questionnaire response.
Hosts and authorized Group managers can view them. Other Readers—and Team Leaders
or Floaters solely because of that role—do not get access to your registration
answers. Staff may also see planning information such as your past Challenge
reading figures and Discord username.

### Edit answers while allowed

Use **Edit Registration** on the Challenge, make your changes and choose
**Save Registration**. Each Challenge sets its own policy: no editing after
registration, a limited number of hours from your registration, or editing until
registration closes. Once your window ends, the answers are locked. Contact a
Host if important information needs attention; do not withdraw just to try to
reset the deadline.

Answer editing does not let you change a confirmed Personal TBR.

### Withdraw if you cannot participate

In Upcoming or Active, use **Withdraw** on the Challenge and read the confirmation.
You leave active participation and your current team, but your submissions and
history are retained. Withdrawal can still be available after registration closes.

If registration is open later, a withdrawn Reader may register again. Previous
answers and confirmed TBR are reused, and their original locks do not reset.
If staff removed your participation, ask a Host to reactivate it instead. A prior
team assignment is not automatically restored.

## Choose a Personal TBR, if you want one

Personal TBR is an optional planned-reading list for a Challenge that enables it.
You can register **up to nine books**. It is not a requirement to use Hardcover
or to participate in the Challenge.

While completing registration:

1. In **Personal TBR**, use **Search Hardcover** when your personal connection is
   available, or **Enter manually**.
2. For manual entries, fill in **Title** and **Author**. **Page Count (Optional)**
   can be left blank. Use **Add Book** for another entry.
3. Use **Move Up**, **Move Down** and **Remove** to organize your list before
   confirming it.
4. Review the list and advertised bonuses on **Registration Summary**.

TBR catalog search uses **your personal Hardcover connection**, not the Group's
shared submission search. You can connect from My Account without enabling library
sync, or simply enter your TBR manually.

**Confirm Registration locks the list, including an empty list.** With no books,
the summary warns that continuing makes you ineligible for Personal TBR bonus
pages in this Challenge. With fewer than nine, it explains that qualifying books
can still earn configured per-book bonuses, but not the full nine-book bonus.
Use Back to Edit before confirming if that is not what you intend.

After confirmation, open **Personal TBR** from the Challenge to see your registered
books and pending/completed progress. Submit completed TBR books through the normal
**Submit Book** form: putting a title on your TBR does not count as reading it.

Each qualifying approved completion can earn the configured per-book bonus.
Partial completion still counts for those individual bonuses. An additional
full-list reward, when offered, requires nine registered books and completion of
all nine. Registration-answer editing and re-registering do not unlock the list.

## Read BOTM books and claim Themes

### Book of the Month

When enabled, **Book of the Month** on the Challenge shows the selected books,
covers, page counts and any bonuses. Open it to see the full list. The current UI
uses book cards/covers, without a separate Table/Posters switch.

Read a listed book, then submit it normally. Its approved reading pages count as
Base; a qualifying BOTM completion can add the advertised per-book bonus. There
may also be a bonus for finishing the full configured set. The list shows your
pending/completed states and completion progress where available. Selecting or
opening a book is not a completion claim by itself.

### Themes

Open **Themes** from the Challenge to read the available Themes, their reading
windows, prompts and bonuses. On your book submission, choose the applicable
**Theme Claims** and answer any attached prompts.

The book's **Completed On** date must fall within that Theme's window. If a Theme
does not allow stacking, you cannot claim it together with another Theme on the
same book. The form will ask you to correct incompatible claims.

Theme bonuses require review. A book may already be Approved while its Theme
claim is still pending; those are separate decisions.

## Submit a completed book

You must be an active participant in an **Active** Challenge. Open that Challenge
and choose **Submit Book**. Hardcover is optional.

### Find the book through shared catalog search

If **Find on Hardcover** is available:

1. Search **Title, Author, or ISBN**, or paste a **Hardcover Book or Edition Link**
   and choose **Import Link**.
2. Choose **View Editions** and select the correct format/edition with
   **Use Edition**.
3. Check the selected title, author and page count in **Book Details**.

Catalog page counts are locked and verified automatically. For an audiobook,
Northbound may show that it is scoring with a page-bearing edition rather than
audio duration. Check the selection message before submitting.

If the title or edition cannot be found, has no usable page count, or search is
unavailable, use manual entry. **Clear and Enter Manually** clears the catalog
selection and book identity/page fields so you can enter the correct information.

### Complete Book Details

For manual entry, supply **Title**, **Author**, **Book Format** and **Submitted
Pages**. Then, for either entry method:

1. Set **Completed On** to the date you finished. It must be within the Challenge
   dates. **Started On** is optional and cannot be later than completion.
2. Add an optional **Reference Link** to the edition or evidence of its page count.
3. Use **Notes** for anything the reviewer should know.
4. Select relevant **Theme Claims** and answer their prompts.
5. Recheck the Challenge, book/edition, dates, pages and claims. Choose **Submit Book**.

There is **no separate final confirmation popup** for a book submission. Submit
Book saves the entry when it is valid. Any field errors must be corrected first.
The blank-TBR warning belongs to registration confirmation, not book submission.

## After you submit

Check your submission on the Challenge or in **My Stats → My Book History**:

- **Pending review:** a manual entry is waiting for staff verification.
- **Approved:** the approved base pages count toward reading totals. A supported
  Hardcover selection is normally approved automatically.
- **Rejected:** the entry does not contribute approved reading pages. Ask a Host
  if you need help understanding or correcting it.

A Host, Floater or appropriate Team Leader may review your activity. Theme claims
can remain pending after the book is approved; check their separate status on
the Challenge. BOTM/TBR completion may also need review before a bonus appears.

Readers do not have a normal edit/delete/resubmit control for an existing book
entry. Contact a Host about a wrong date, page count, title or rejected submission
rather than creating a duplicate to correct it. **Needs Attention** is a staff
review area, not your personal submission inbox.

## Understand your score and history

**Base pages + bonuses/modifiers = final score.** For example, 300 approved pages
and a 50-page Theme bonus produce a score of 350. Enter the book's pages, not
350, as Submitted Pages.

On the Challenge's submission table you may see **Submitted**, **Base**, **Bonus**,
**Final** and **Status**. Reports use **Base**, **Modifier** and **Total** for the
same distinction between reading and scoring. Bonuses can come from approved
Themes, qualifying BOTM or Personal TBR completions, full-list rewards and Games.
Some apply to your whole Challenge score rather than one book.

### My Stats

Open **My Stats** from your account menu for your private Northbound history:

- **Verified Pages**, **Approved Books**, **Groups** and **Months Participated**.
- **Group History** and **Monthly History**, including your recorded team where
  applicable, approved books and page totals.
- **My Book History**, with completion date, book, Group/Challenge, format, status,
  pages and verification method.

Despite the label, **Verified Pages** in these summary/history totals includes
scoring modifiers; it is not strictly base reading pages. Individual book-history
rows show approved base pages, or submitted pages for a book not yet approved.
That difference can explain why adding up book rows does not match a summary total.

From a Challenge participant or team list, selecting your own name opens your
Participant Detail. Your feature progress and submissions appear there; score
and modifier sections depend on the Challenge's competition visibility settings.
Viewing another Reader's detailed page may be restricted even if you can see
their name or score.

### Your team and competition information

Open **Teams**, then your team to see its current roster. Team Leaders appear
first, followed by the other Readers; the default order is alphabetical within
each group. Contact a Host about assignment problems—you do not move yourself
between teams.

Team totals, **Team Comparison**, Reader scores and some planning/progress columns
depend on competition visibility. Hidden information is not a zero score or an
error. If reporting filters are available, check **From**, **To** and **Show
Bonuses**, then use **Apply Filters**. **Reset Filters** helps you compare totals
on the same basis. A filtered **Displayed Team Total** need not match an unfiltered
Challenge report.

### Games and checkpoints

Open **Games** when enabled to see your Challenge's activities. Rewards may be
given to you individually, your team or the whole Challenge. They add modifier
pages, not books or base reading pages. Where your Participant Detail shows
**Modifiers / Rewards**, an active Games reward includes its source, reason/context,
amount and effective date.

If a reward is voided, its score effect is removed. Staff retain the award and
void history, but Readers do not have that full management-history screen.
Ask a Host if a reward or total needs explanation.

Some Challenges use progress checkpoints—for example, reaching 25% of a reading
target, measured with base pages or total score. Results are captured at a point
in time and later score changes do not rewrite them. The warning/results screens
are staff-facing, not a Reader checkpoint dashboard. A Host may follow up with
you; checkpoints do not automatically deduct pages or remove you.

## Connect your own Hardcover account, optionally

You can register and submit manually without Hardcover. You may also see shared
Hardcover search for submissions or BOTM without having a personal connection.
That shared catalog access does **not** authorize changes to your Hardcover library.
Personal TBR search uses your separate personal connection.

### Recommended: OAuth when available

1. Open **My Account → Personal Hardcover Connection**.
2. Choose **Connect with Hardcover**.
3. Review and approve the request on Hardcover, then return to Northbound.
4. Check for **Connected** and the granted permissions.

Connecting alone does not enable sync or change your library. If already connected,
the action may be **Reconnect / Update Permissions** or **Switch to OAuth**.
Your current connection remains active unless the replacement succeeds.

### Advanced: personal scoped API key

If OAuth is unavailable or you prefer a key, expand **Advanced: scoped API key**.
Paste your own **Personal Hardcover API Token** and choose **Connect with Scoped
API Key**. The key should include `read:catalog`, `read:library` and `write:library`.
Keep it private; do not send it to a Host or use somebody else's key.

Existing connections show **Replace Scoped API Key** or **Switch to Scoped API
Key** as appropriate. A failed replacement does not replace the working connection.
Northbound cannot inspect this key's scopes or expiry, so a successful connection
test does not guarantee every sync permission. Use **Test Saved Connection** to
check access, or replace an expired/restricted key here.

### Choose what you want synchronized

Under **Hardcover Synchronization**, both preferences default **OFF**:

- **Sync completed books:** eligible new approvals can be marked Read in your
  personal Hardcover library.
- **Sync Northbound completion dates:** also sends the submission's actual finish
  date as its own read occurrence. Distinct Northbound rereads can remain distinct
  occurrences. This option requires Sync completed books to be enabled.

Choose only the options you want, then **Save Synchronization Preferences**.
Connecting is not consent. Nobody else can enable these preferences for you.
If **Synchronization unavailable** is shown, follow its explanation before trying
to enable sync. Northbound will not substitute a Group connection for yours.

**Sync is forward-only.** It applies to eligible new approvals after you enable
consent. It does not export previously approved Challenge history, backfill old
submissions or import your Hardcover reading history. There is no historical
backfill feature. If you want a future book approval considered for sync, enable
consent before that approval—not afterward.

Synchronization runs in the background and not every entry is eligible. A safe
Hardcover book match is needed; manual or unmatched entries are not a promise of
automatic export. External availability does not stop Northbound approval or scoring.

### Check synchronization health

My Account shows **Synchronization health**, including these counts:

- **Pending:** work is waiting to be processed.
- **Retry scheduled:** a temporary problem will be tried again when eligible.
- **Reconnect required:** repair your personal connection, using the reconnect
  or replacement control shown above.
- **Reconciliation required:** a previous result is uncertain; it needs review
  rather than blindly sending it again.
- **Recovery review:** work is held for safety and needs review before proceeding.

**Last success** and **Last safe issue**, when present, give recent context.
**Sync Now** wakes eligible work that already exists. It does not scan old
submissions or create historical work, and it cannot bypass reconciliation or
recovery holds. If a review-required status persists, contact a Host for help
getting it investigated; never share your token. Repeatedly clicking Sync Now
does not make uncertain work safe to repeat.

### Disconnect or reconnect

In **Personal Hardcover Connection**, **Disconnect** acts immediately; there is
no separate confirmation page. It removes Northbound's saved personal connection
and switches both sync preferences OFF. Your Northbound submissions, reading
history and confirmed TBR are retained.

Disconnecting does not delete reading already sent to Hardcover. Reconnecting
does not bulk-export history or automatically turn consent back on after a
disconnect. Review and save your preferences again if you want future sync.
Existing eligible unfinished work may resume once the connection and consent
allow it; that is different from scanning old approved books.

## A simple participation routine

1. Find your Challenge and check its dates and registration status.
2. Register; add an optional TBR **before** confirming if you want one.
3. Check your team assignment and read the available Themes/BOTM activities.
4. Read, then submit completed books while the Challenge is Active.
5. Include applicable Theme claims and responses with each submission.
6. Follow review status, your score and any team information available to you.
7. Join optional Games or respond to Host progress check-ins as you wish.
8. If you want personal Hardcover sync, connect and enable consent before the
   new approvals you want synchronized. It is never required for participation.

## Common questions

**Register or Submit Book is missing.** Check the Challenge stage and registration
status. Submitting requires active participation and Active stage. Ask a Host if
you were removed or your participation needs checking.

**I cannot edit my answers or TBR.** Answer windows are Challenge-specific.
Confirmed TBR is locked even if answers remain editable; re-registering does not
reset either lock.

**Hardcover cannot find my book.** Try the correct title/author/ISBN or edition
link, then use manual entry if needed. Submission search and personal TBR search
use different connections, so one can work while the other is unavailable.

**My bonus is missing.** Check the book and claim status, configured reward and
completion requirements. Partial TBR completion can earn per-book bonuses, not
the full nine-book reward. Ask a Host about a pending completion match.

**My score or team information looks different.** Compare Base versus Final/Total,
date ranges and Show Bonuses. Visibility restrictions can hide some competition
information; My Stats remains your private history view.

**My book was rejected or has incorrect details.** Contact a Host with the book
and Challenge name. Do not create duplicate submissions just to fix an existing one.

**Personal sync is unavailable or waiting.** Read the My Account connection and
health messages. Reconnect if requested; use Sync Now only for existing eligible
work. A failed external sync does not mean your Northbound score was rejected.
