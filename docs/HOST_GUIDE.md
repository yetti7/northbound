# Host Guide

As a Host, you configure and run a particular Challenge: prepare registration,
organize teams and review support, verify reading, and bring the Challenge to a
clear finish. Your Host assignment belongs to that Challenge, not to the Group
permanently. If you are also competing, you register and join a team separately.

## Find your working areas

Open your Challenge from its Group. A new Host assignment may also appear in
**Needs Attention**, with **Open Challenge Settings** as a shortcut.

On the Challenge page:

- **Challenge Schedule** shows registration status and key dates.
- **Challenge Settings** holds configuration, optional features and lifecycle actions.
- **Challenge Participants** is your registration and team-planning starting point.
- **Teams** opens rosters and reports; **Team Comparison** summarizes team scores.
- **Review Queue** opens pending book and Theme review work.
- **Themes**, **Book of the Month** and **Games** lead to enabled activities.
  Optional-feature setup links appear when those features are not enabled.
- The submissions section lets you inspect Challenge reading. Personal TBR and
  personal completion displays are tied to the Reader viewing the page; use
  Participant Detail to inspect someone else's progress.

Available actions change with the Challenge stage. Missing setup controls in
Finalizing or Completed do not necessarily mean your Host assignment was lost.

## Before registration: prepare the Challenge

The Group Owner arranges Challenge creation and Host assignments. If you need
another Host, ask them or someone delegated that Group responsibility. Being a
Host alone does not let you appoint Hosts, change permanent Group roles or manage
the Group's Hardcover connection.

Within your Challenge, use **Challenge Settings** to manage general details,
schedule, registration, announcements, checkpoints, competition visibility,
teams, Floaters, Themes, BOTM, Games and lifecycle actions. You can see current
Hosts from **Challenge Staff**, but the **Hosts** assignment control requires
separate Group-management authority.

### Title, description and announcement

Open the **General** card, edit **Challenge Title** and the description, then
choose **Save General Settings**. Use **Challenge Announcement** for a message
specific to this event. Group-wide announcements are a separate responsibility.

### Set dates and automatic actions

1. Open **Challenge Schedule**.
2. Set **Registration Opens**, **Registration Closes**, **Challenge Starts** and
   **Challenge Ends**. The page tells you the Group timezone being used.
3. Check the automatic-action box beside each date you want Northbound to act on.
4. Optionally set **Final Announcement** and **Mark Challenge Completed at this
   time**. Leave automatic completion off if you want to finish review manually.
5. Choose **Save Schedule**.

Entering dates without their corresponding automatic-action boxes does not enable
those actions. Opening registration automatically also publishes a Draft as
Upcoming. Starting moves Upcoming to Active; ending moves Active to Finalizing.
Automatic completion moves Finalizing to Completed—it is not a check that your
review queue is clear.

## Set up and open registration

Registration has its own Open/Closed status. It is not the same thing as the
Challenge's lifecycle stage.

Use **Registration Opens** with **Open registration automatically**, and
**Registration Closes** with **Close registration automatically**. Check the
Challenge page after the scheduled time: Readers need both open registration and
an **Upcoming** or **Active** Challenge to register.

If you use **Move Forward to Upcoming** to publish early, registration does not
open just because the stage changed. There is no separate manual Open Registration
button in the current settings page; use the schedule and its action checkboxes.

### Questions and the answer-editing window

1. Open **Challenge Settings → Signup Questions**.
2. Use **Add Question** for any information you need. You can have up to ten
   custom questions; none are required just to run registration.
3. Set each question's wording, type and required status. Supply choices for
   choice questions. Use **Move Up**, **Move Down** or **Remove Question** as needed.
4. Set **Reader Answer Editing**:

   - **No editing after registration** locks answers on registration.
   - **Editing allowed for a set period** uses **Editing Duration (Hours)**,
     measured from that Reader's registration.
   - **Editing allowed until registration closes** follows the registration gate.

5. Choose **Save Registration Settings**.

Readers still complete a registration confirmation when you have no questions.
Once responses have been collected, question structure and the editing policy
are locked. Decide these before inviting registrations. A timed answer-editing
window is not the same as the window for new registrations.

Hosts can read responses, but do not have a control to rewrite another Reader's
answers. Ask the Reader to correct them while their own editing window is open.
Do not collect information you do not need, or share private registration answers
as part of public team announcements.

### Configure Personal TBR alongside registration

In **Signup Questions → Personal TBR**:

1. Select **Enable Personal TBR** if your Challenge will use it.
2. Set **Per-book TBR Bonus Pages** and, optionally,
   **Full 9-Book TBR Completion Bonus Pages**.
3. Choose **Save Personal TBR Settings**.

Readers may prepare up to nine books during registration. The list locks when
registration is confirmed. An answer-editing window does **not** unlock the TBR,
and a Host cannot simply rewrite the confirmed list for a Reader.

Approved qualifying completions earn the configured per-book bonus. The full-list
bonus requires a nine-book list and completion of all nine; a shorter completed
list does not earn that bonus. View the Reader's list, pending/completed states
and earned modifiers in Participant Detail. See [resolve completion matches](#resolve-botm-and-personal-tbr-matches)
when an approved book has not contributed as expected.

## During registration: plan participants and teams

### Use Challenge Participants

Open **Challenge Participants**. Compare **Reader**, **Discord Username**,
**Avg Pages**, **Last Challenge**, **Completed**, **Team** and **Registration**.
The sortable headings let you compare reading history or find unassigned Readers.

Planning figures come from eligible Completed Challenges in this Group and use
approved base pages—not current scores with bonuses. Missing history is not zero
ability. Use these figures as context alongside registration responses; Northbound
does not choose your community's team-balancing policy.

Select a Reader's name for Participant Detail. It brings together **Score**,
**Planning / History**, **Registration**, **Personal TBR**, **Book of the Month**,
**Themes**, **Submissions** and **Modifiers / Rewards**, where applicable. This is
the place to read their answers and inspect feature completion, not just their
overall score. Treat Discord usernames and planning data as operational information;
the Reader controls whether their username is public.

### Add, remove or reactivate a participant

- **Add Participant** lets you choose an eligible Group member and an optional
  team, then **Save**. This adds participation; it does not complete registration
  answers or a TBR on their behalf. Someone missing from the Group must first
  join through the Group's normal membership process.
- **Remove** on a participant ends active participation and their current team
  and Team Leader assignments, while retaining submissions and history. Read the
  confirmation and record a useful reason.
- **Reactivate** brings an inactive participant back, but does not restore their
  previous team or Team Leader role. Assign those again if appropriate.

Readers can withdraw themselves in Upcoming or Active, even when registration
is closed. A withdrawn Reader is shown as inactive, with their history preserved.
Someone removed by staff needs a Host to reactivate them; they cannot override
that removal by registering again. These normal Host roster changes are available
in Draft, Upcoming and Active, not as an unrestricted late-stage correction tool.

### Create teams and assign Readers

1. Open **Teams → Add Team**.
2. Set the team's **Name** and **Color**, then **Save**.
3. In **Challenge Participants**, use **Edit Team** beside a Reader, choose their
   team and **Save**. You can also choose a team when adding a participant.
4. Open the team to check its **Current Roster**.

Moving a Reader ends their old assignment and creates their current placement;
the previous team history remains. Any Team Leader role on the old team ends too.
Assign leadership on the new team deliberately if needed. Removing a Reader from
a team is different from removing them from Challenge participation.

The default roster puts **Team Leaders first**, alphabetically within that group,
then the other Readers alphabetically. Sorting by score or progress still keeps
the two role groups separate.

Use **Edit Team → Save Team** to change its name or color. **Archive Team** hides
it from active views and selectors but preserves its roster and history. Find it
under **View Archive**, then use **Restore Team** when needed. **Delete Team** is
only offered for an unused team in Draft and permanently removes that team.

### Assign review support

For a competing Reader who will review their own team:

1. Open the team's **Manage Team Leaders**.
2. Choose an eligible current team Reader and **Assign Team Leader**.
3. Use **Remove Team Leader** when that responsibility ends. **Team Leader History**
   remains available.

Team Leaders can review books and Theme claims, and review Personal TBR matches,
within their current team scope. They do not become permanent Group managers or
gain your Challenge-wide configuration authority.

For Challenge-wide review support, open **Challenge Settings → Floaters**, select
an eligible Member and **Assign Floater**. **Remove Floater** ends the assignment;
**Floater History** preserves it. Floaters are non-competing: they cannot also
have active Reader participation or a team in that Challenge. Resolve the existing
participation/assignment first rather than treating Floater as an extra team label.
Floaters can review across teams, but are not substitutes for Hosts managing setup,
Games or BOTM match decisions.

## Review books and Theme claims

### Start with Needs Attention or Review Queue

**Needs Attention** groups work by Challenge. It can include new Host-assignment
notices, pending submissions, pending Theme claims and below-target checkpoint
warnings. Its number is not a count of unique books: one book can have several
claims. Use **Open Review Queue** for review work or **Open Challenge Participants**
for checkpoint follow-up.

The Challenge's **Review Queue** shows pending books and books with pending Theme
claims, even if the book itself is already approved. Open a book card to review it.
Hosts see the whole Challenge; Team Leaders see only their current review scope.
Normal review is available in **Active** and **Finalizing**.

### Make a review decision

1. In **Submitted Book**, read the Reader, title, author, format, completion date,
   **Submitted Pages**, reference link and **Reader Notes**.
2. In **Decision**, enter **Approved Pages** for a manual book. Use the verified
   count, not a bonus-inclusive score. Add an optional **Verification Link**.
3. Choose the submission **Status**. An Approved book needs a positive approved
   page count; use Rejected when it should not count, with clear **Review Notes**.
4. Review each **Theme Claims** response and set that claim's status separately.
5. Choose **Save Review**, then check the resulting score/progress when necessary.

Approving the book alone does not approve its pending Theme claims. Rejecting
the book rejects its claims and removes their scoring contribution. Pending or
rejected books do not contribute approved reading pages. Your decision and notes
do not replace the Reader's original submitted page count or entry.

### Understand catalog pages versus manual pages

A supported Hardcover selection with a usable scoring page count is automatically
verified and approved. Its catalog metadata supplies the base pages; the selected
audio edition may use a separate page-bearing scoring edition. Such a book may
still appear in the queue because its Theme claims need your decision.

**Hardcover-verified page counts are locked.** The ordinary review form cannot
override those Approved Pages or reject the catalog-verified book. You can still
review its Theme claims. Manual entries instead use your approved count. The
review page shows Submitted Pages and Current Score; it is not a metadata editor.

For an incorrect manual decision, reopen the book from Participant Detail's
**Submissions**, adjust the decision and **Save Review** while review is allowed.
For a book that needs removal, the Challenge submissions section offers Host-only
**Remove** during the configurable stages. Confirm carefully: it disappears from
active totals, but its record and removal history are retained. Removal is not
available in Finalizing simply because review is still open. There is no Host
control here to edit a Reader's title or completion date.

### Read the score correctly

**Base pages + modifiers/bonus pages = final score.** Reports label these
**Base**, **Modifier** and **Total**; the review screen calls the result total in
**Current Score**. For example, 300 approved pages plus a 50-page Theme reward
produces 350 total pages. Do not enter 350 in Approved Pages in that example.

Modifiers can include approved Theme bonuses, qualifying BOTM and TBR rewards,
full-list completion rewards and Games. A Games reward is not another book or
additional base reading. Participant Detail's **Modifiers / Rewards** explains
the active sources behind the Reader's overall modifier total; not every reward
belongs to a single submission.

### Hardcover personal sync needs no Host action

If the Reader independently connected Hardcover and enabled personal sync, a new
eligible approval may queue a background library update. Do nothing extra in
review. Provider availability does not block Northbound approval or scoring.
Only the Reader controls their credentials and consent; the Group key cannot
replace their connection. Previously approved history is not backfilled when
they turn sync on. Do not reapprove books just to try to trigger external sync.

## Configure and review Themes

1. Open **Themes → Add Theme**.
2. Enter **Name**, **Description**, **Starts On**, **Ends On** and **Bonus Pages**.
3. Add a **Prompt** if a Reader must explain their claim. A selected Theme with a
   prompt requires an answer during submission.
4. Set **Allow Stacking**, **Active** and **Visible to Readers**, then **Save**.

The book's completion date must fall inside the Theme's reading window. If a
selected Theme does not allow stacking, it cannot be claimed alongside another
Theme on the same book. Active and visible are separate: you can prepare a hidden
Theme without showing it to Readers. Hosts can see inactive and hidden Themes.

Use **Edit Theme** for changes while setup is allowed. Claim approval supplies
the bonus only when the book is approved too. Previously awarded Theme amounts
are preserved; editing the advertised bonus is not a bulk rewrite of earlier
awards. Review the Reader's response in Submission Review, not just the Theme title.

## Manage Book of the Month

### Enable and build the list

1. In **Challenge Settings → BOTM Settings**, select **Enable Book of the Month**.
2. Set **Full BOTM Completion Bonus Pages** if completing the whole configured
   list should earn an extra reward; use zero for none. Save the settings.
3. Open **Books**, or **Book of the Month** on the Challenge, then **Add Book**.
4. Use **Find on Hardcover** with **Search** or **Import Link** to select a title,
   or **Clear and Enter Manually** to enter it yourself.
5. Check **Position**, **Title**, **Author**, **Page Count** and optional
   **Per-book Bonus Pages**. Choose **Save Book**.

A populated list can contain one to nine active books. Bonus pages may be zero.
The current list uses book cards/covers; it has no separate Table/Posters switch.
Catalog-linked identity fields may be locked when editing an imported book.
Ask the Group Owner or delegated connection manager if shared Hardcover search
needs attention; manual entry remains available.

### Maintain the list and check awards

Use **Edit** on a book to change permitted settings. **Retire** removes it from
the active list while preserving its history; **Reactivate** returns a retired
book to an available position. **Delete** is for unused configuration and is
blocked when history requires preservation. It does not delete the catalog book.
These configuration actions are for Draft, Upcoming and Active.

Approved qualifying books contribute per-book bonuses. Completing the configured
set may earn the full-list reward. The awarded set and amount are retained in
**Match History → Completion Awards**, so later list changes are not a reason
to assume earlier awards have been rewritten. Check **Currently qualifying**
versus historical/inactive records before explaining a Reader's total.

### Resolve BOTM and Personal TBR matches

A pending match asks whether the approved submission is the same book as the
listed candidate. It is separate from approving the book's page count.

- For BOTM, open **Book of the Month → Match History**. Hosts make these decisions.
- For TBR, open **Review Queue → Personal TBR Matches**. Hosts, Floaters and
  Team Leaders can act within their review scope.

Compare the submission, candidate and displayed evidence. Use **Confirm** only
when they are the same book, or **Reject** when they are not. Do not confirm an
uncertain candidate merely to award a bonus. **Synchronize** rechecks Northbound
completion matches; it is not a request to sync the Reader's Hardcover library.

Return to Participant Detail to check completed/pending states and active rewards.
Qualifying TBR bonuses contribute to current scores.

## Run Games and apply manual rewards

### Create an optional activity

Enable **Games** in Challenge Settings, then open **Games → Add Game**. Set
**Name**, **Starts At**, **Ends At** and **Maximum Bonus Pages**, and save. The
maximum is the limit for an individual reward application, not an automatic
award. A Game's schedule describes the activity; applying a reward is a deliberate
Host action, available when Games are enabled, the Game is active and the Challenge
is Active or Finalizing.

### Apply a reward

1. Open the Game and choose **Apply Reward**.
2. Enter **Amount** and choose **Apply To**: **Individual**, **Team**, or
   **Challenge-wide** (the entire eligible Challenge).
3. Select the Reader or team where required and enter **Reason / Note**.
4. Review **Confirm Reward**, especially its recipient list.
5. Choose **Confirm and Apply Reward** only when the amount and recipients are right.

The amount is awarded to each eligible recipient, not divided among them. The
recipient list is fixed when you apply it, so later team changes do not silently
move the reward to other Readers. Its effective date is the Group-local date of
application; the form does not offer backdating. These pages are modifiers, not
book submissions.

### Correct a reward without hiding what happened

Open the Game's **Reward History** to inspect the amount, reason, person who
applied it and **Frozen Recipients**. For a mistaken application, choose **Void
Reward**, provide the reason and **Confirm Void Reward**. Voiding removes its
score effect but preserves the application, recipients and explanation. If a
replacement is needed, apply a new correct reward when permitted.

**Retire Game** stops new awards from that Game, without erasing existing rewards.
**Reactivate Game** makes it available again when other conditions allow.
**Delete Unused Game** is only for a Game without reward history. Disabling the
Challenge's Games feature likewise preserves existing rewards and scores.
Readers can see their resulting scores; detailed reward-management history is
for Hosts, not a public edit log.

## Set checkpoints and follow up on progress

In **Challenge Settings → Progress Checkpoints**, use **Add Checkpoint** to
configure up to five checks. Set:

- **Checkpoint Date and Time** and **Threshold Percentage**.
- **Progress Basis**: **Base Pages** or **Total Pages**.
- **Target Basis**: **Previous Monthly Average** or **Fixed Target**. For the
  latter, enter **Fixed Target Pages**.

Choose **Save Checkpoints**. For example, a 25% threshold against a fixed
1,000-page target asks whether the Reader has reached 250 pages on the chosen basis.

When a due checkpoint is captured during Active, it records that moment's progress
and outcome for active Readers. Missing previous history can mean Not evaluated.
Below-target results appear in **Needs Attention** and **Challenge Participants**.
They do not remove Readers, deduct pages or automatically change team assignments.

Once evaluation begins, configuration locks. Later approvals or score corrections
do not silently rewrite the captured checkpoint. A check not processed until
after Active is skipped, not reconstructed as if it had run earlier. If the
screen shows **Recovery Hold**, automatic evaluation is paused; do not treat an
old warning or held check as a fresh assessment.

## Monitor reports and decide who sees competition results

Use **Challenge Participants** for registration/history planning and select a
Reader for their detailed score, submissions and feature progress. Use **Teams**
and **Team Comparison** for standings, then open a team to investigate its total.

In Team Detail:

1. Set **From** and **To** within the Challenge dates when you need a date range.
2. Choose **Show Bonuses** to include modifiers, or clear it for base-only reporting.
3. Use **Show Columns** for available Discord and historical planning figures.
4. Use **Show Readers** to filter Themes, TBR and BOTM completion. TBR also allows
   finding Readers without a list.
5. Choose **Apply Filters**; use **Reset Filters** before comparing with an
   unfiltered total. Sort by the available headings, or **Apply Sort** on mobile.

Date filters use book completion dates and reward effective dates, not simply
the day you reviewed a book. Feature completion indicators are overall Challenge
progress, not a fresh completion calculation restricted to the selected dates.
**Displayed Team Total** reflects the displayed/filtered Readers and selected
date/bonus options. **Current Readers** counts the whole current roster.

If the numbers differ, compare like with like: Base with Base, Total with Total,
the same dates and the same Readers. Historical average pages are a planning
figure, not another version of the current score. Participant Detail's active
modifier list is often the quickest explanation for an unexpected total.

Use **Challenge Settings → Competition Visibility** to set **Reader Scores**
and **Team Standings** separately. Choose the audience your community expects,
from **Hosts only** through team-limited options to **Everybody**. Everybody
means Group members, not anonymous visitors. Hosts retain oversight visibility;
seeing scores does not by itself grant a Reader review authority.

## End the Challenge in the right stage

The current stage labels are **Draft → Upcoming → Active → Finalizing → Completed
→ Archived**. Registration Open/Closed is shown separately. What you may call
“closing the Challenge” is the move to **Finalizing**, not a stage named Closed.

| Stage | What to do as Host |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | Prepare settings and activities. Ordinary Members cannot see this preparation space. |
| Upcoming | Members can see the Challenge. Take registrations when its registration gate is open; organize teams. No book submissions yet. |
| Active | Readers with active participation can submit books. Review, monitor reports and apply rewards. Registration may be open or closed independently. |
| Finalizing | New book submissions and self-registration are closed. Finish outstanding reviews and manual rewards; most normal setup/roster controls are no longer available. |
| Completed | Results are ready; normal book review is no longer available. Keep the Challenge for reference. |
| Archived | Put the Challenge away while retaining history. There is no normal backward lifecycle action from Archived. |

Hosts can use the available **Move Forward to** and **Move Back to** actions in
**Challenge Settings → Challenge Lifecycle**. Moves are between adjacent stages.
Backward changes require confirmation; Completed to Finalizing uses **Confirm
Recovery to Finalizing** and can change results visibility without deleting records.
Check scheduled actions before moving backward so automation does not immediately
move it forward again. A backward move does not automatically unlock questions,
confirmed TBRs or captured checkpoints.

Before moving to Completed, clear pending book/Theme reviews, check unresolved
completion matches, verify Games rewards, and compare unfiltered totals. Coordinate
timing with other Hosts and the Group Owner. Archive only when you are ready for
the lack of a normal return action.

## A simple running routine

1. **Before registration:** agree rules; check schedule, questions/edit policy,
   optional TBR, Themes, BOTM and checkpoints. Arrange review support.
2. **Registration period:** verify Open status, monitor participants and answers,
   plan teams, and assign Team Leaders and separate Floaters.
3. **Active:** check Needs Attention, review books and claims, resolve completion
   matches, apply agreed Games rewards and monitor filtered reports/checkpoints.
4. **Ending:** move to Finalizing to stop new submissions, finish review and rewards,
   verify totals, then move to Completed. Archive later when appropriate.

Optional features are optional; a Challenge does not need all of them to run well.

## Common problems

**A Reader cannot register.** Check active Group membership, registration Open
status and Upcoming/Active stage. A staff-removed participant needs **Reactivate**.
An active Floater must stop that assignment before competing.

**A Reader cannot edit registration.** Check their chosen answer-editing policy
and registration time. Host access to answers does not let you change them.
Confirmed Personal TBR is locked separately.

**A Team Leader cannot review someone.** Confirm both are on the appropriate
current team, the leader's assignment is current, and review is still Active or
Finalizing. Moving a leader ends their former team leadership.

**The queue still shows an approved book.** Inspect its pending Theme claims.
Catalog verification approves base reading, not the Reader's Theme responses.

**A BOTM/TBR bonus is missing.** Check feature enablement, configured bonus,
approved/nonremoved submission and qualifying completion. Inspect Match History
for a pending decision. A full nine-book TBR bonus does not apply to a shorter
list; BOTM full completion uses its configured set. Check active modifiers rather
than assuming a matching title alone guarantees an award.

**Hardcover search fails.** Ask the Group connection manager to check shared
catalog access, or use manual entry. Personal TBR lookup and personal library
sync use the Reader's connection; the Reader must handle their own connection
problem. Their sync failure does not require reversing a Northbound approval.

**A scheduled change did not give the expected result.** Check the displayed
Group timezone, time, action checkbox and starting stage. Publishing Upcoming
does not by itself open registration. Due actions need time to be processed;
check the displayed stage and registration status again before announcing a change.

**A setting is missing or locked.** Check your current assignment and the page's
stage/lock message. Ask the Group Owner about new Host assignments or Group-only
settings; do not change a Reader's permanent role to solve a Challenge task.
For general Group responsibilities, see the [Group Owner Guide](GROUP_OWNER_GUIDE.md).
