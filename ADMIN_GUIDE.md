## 1. Understanding Northbound

Northbound is a self-hosted home for a reading community's Challenges. It brings registration, teams, completed-book submissions, staff review and scores into one place instead of separate forms and spreadsheets. It does not manage Discord membership or automatically create accounts from Discord. A Discord username is optional profile information.

The system fits together like this:

- An **account** signs in to an installation.
- A **Group** is a persistent community, with its own membership, timezone, invitation code and optional shared Hardcover catalog connection.
- A **Challenge** belongs to one Group. It has its own schedule, registration, staffing, teams and optional activities.
- A **Reader** is an active participant in that Challenge. Group membership alone is not participation.
- A **team** groups Challenge Readers. Staff place Readers and appoint Team Leaders.
- A **submission** records a completed book. Approved, non-removed submissions contribute base reading pages. Pending or rejected books do not.
- **Modifiers** add eligible Theme, BOTM, Personal TBR or Games rewards. **Base + Modifier = Total**.

Northbound reading history is generated by Challenge participation. It is not a general lifetime book tracker, and it does not import a Reader's Hardcover history. Progress displays combine approved reading, optional activity completion and available scores. Checkpoints are staff snapshots against configured targets, not daily reading-entry forms.

Hardcover is optional. Shared catalog search can verify editions and pages; manual entries can be reviewed by staff. A Reader's separate personal connection supports Personal TBR lookup and expressly consented future library updates. Connecting is not consent.

Administrators prepare account access, Group rules, staff, dates, registration and scoring; Hosts operate events and review mistakes; Team Leaders and Floaters provide scoped review support. Finish outstanding review before announcing final results.

## 2. Account roles and permissions

There are three separate layers: installation authority, persistent Group roles, and Challenge-specific staffing/participation. They do not form a single ladder in which every senior title inherits every lower role.

### Roles in practice

| Role | How assigned and scope | What it permits | Limits and relevant screens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Platform Owner** | Initial `/setup/` account or accepted invitation from another Platform Owner; entire installation | Platform Administration, account/Group management, Challenge operation and review overrides, recovery, maintenance, backups | Not automatically a Group member, Host, Reader or scoring participant. Use a separate normal account for community participation. Normal participation excludes Platform Owner accounts. |
| **Group Owner** | Normal account creates a Group, or another authorized person changes its Persistent Group Role | By default Group settings/integration, permanent membership, Challenge creation/Host appointment, Group announcements and permission delegation; Challenge configuration/lifecycle and score oversight | Not automatically a Host or reviewer. Team operations, Themes, BOTM and Games operation require Host assignment. Participant Detail is also available through scoped TBR-management authority; answer and score permissions remain separate. |
| **Moderator** | Persistent Group Role set through Members → Permissions | Default extra capabilities: Group announcements and Challenge TBR assistance within their Group. May receive specific delegated capabilities | Does not automatically administer membership, create Challenges, appoint Hosts or review books. Competition oversight has additional conditions below. |
| **Member** | Joins with code, or is created using Add Member | Group access and visible Challenges; can register when eligible | No default management/review authority. May receive delegated Group capabilities. |
| **Host** | Authorized Group Challenge manager assigns an active normal Group member for one Challenge | Challenge settings, teams/rosters, Themes, BOTM, Games, Floaters, Challenge announcements, lifecycle and Challenge-wide review | Does not grant permanent Group management or Host-appointment authority. Does not register the Host as a Reader. |
| **Team Leader** | Host appoints an already enrolled Reader currently on that team | Reviews books, Theme claims and TBR matches for current Readers on that team; own Reader participation continues | No general settings, roster-management or Group rights. BOTM match decisions are Host operations. Moving/leaving the team ends its leadership. |
| **Floater** | Host assigns an eligible normal Group member in one Challenge | Challenge-wide book/Theme/TBR-match review | Non-competing, no active Reader enrollment or team in that Challenge. Not a Host; no setup, team-management, BOTM-match or submission rights from this role. Score visibility is separately configured. |
| **Reader** | Confirms registration or is added/reactivated by a Host | Submits own completed books while actively participating in an Active Challenge; views own available progress/history | Cannot approve books, move themselves between teams, change locked TBR, or edit/delete submitted entries through the normal Reader UI. |

### Permissions comparison

“Delegated” means a Group capability must actually be allowed. Host and reviewer actions also depend on lifecycle and record eligibility.

| Action | Platform Owner | Group Owner, defaults | Moderator, defaults | Member / Reader | Host | Team Leader | Floater |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Installation administration | Yes | No | No | No | No | No | No |
| Group settings, code and Group token | Yes | Yes | Delegated | Delegated | Separate Group permission | Separate Group permission | Separate Group permission |
| Permanent members / permissions | Yes | Yes | Delegated | Delegated | Separate Group permission | Separate Group permission | Separate Group permission |
| Group announcements | Yes | Yes | Yes | Delegated | Separate Group permission | Separate Group permission | Separate Group permission |
| Create Challenges / appoint Hosts | Yes | Yes | Delegated | Delegated | Separate Group permission | Separate Group permission | Separate Group permission |
| Challenge general/schedule, signup, visibility, lifecycle | Yes | Yes | Delegated Challenge management | Delegated Challenge management | Yes | No | No |
| Read registration answers / planning history | Yes | Yes | Delegated Challenge management | Delegated Challenge management | Yes | No from role alone | No from role alone |
| Teams, enrollment operations, Themes, BOTM, Games | Yes | Requires Host | Requires Host | Requires Host | Yes | No | No |
| Review submissions / Theme claims / TBR matches | Whole Challenge | Requires review staffing | Requires review staffing | Requires review staffing | Whole Challenge | Own current team | Whole Challenge |
| Manage another Reader's Challenge TBR | No from Platform role alone | Own Group | Own Group | No | Assigned Challenge | Own current team | Assigned Challenge |
| Adjudicate BOTM matches | Yes | Requires Host | Requires Host | Requires Host | Yes | No | No |
| Submit completed books | No normal Reader participation | Register separately | Register separately | Active Reader required | Register separately | As Reader | No |

Group permissions are per membership, using **Use Role Default**, **Allow** or **Deny**. An override takes precedence over the role default, including an Owner's defaults. Exposed permissions cover Group settings/access code/integrations, permanent members, Challenge creation/Drafts/Hosts, Group announcements, and Group roles/delegation. Historical compatibility permissions are not a second current score-visibility system.

One account can have different roles in multiple Groups, and different staffing in different Challenges. A Group Owner or Moderator can also be a Host or Team Leader and Reader when eligible. Host appointment itself does not create enrollment. Floater staffing must be resolved before activating Reader participation. A current Floater cannot simultaneously be a Host or Team Leader in the same Challenge; end the conflicting assignment first. Keep Platform Owner and normal participation accounts separate.

### Visibility is not permission to review

**Competition Visibility** has independent **Reader Scores** and **Team Standings** settings. Both default to **Hosts only**. Available choices are **Hosts only**, **Hosts + Floaters**, **Hosts + Team Leaders**, **Team Members**, and **Everybody**. Everybody means authenticated members of the Group, not the public internet. Team Leader/Team Members choices are evaluated for the relevant team, not all teams.

Platform Owners, Group Owners and Hosts have competition oversight. A Moderator receives that oversight when they have the Challenge-management capability. Ordinary delegated members do not acquire all Owner-specific visibility exceptions merely by receiving a capability. Review staffing and registration-answer access are checked separately. A visible score does not grant access to another Reader's full Participant Detail; self, appropriate review authority or scoped TBR-management
authority is required. Answer and score access remain separately checked.

## 3. Initial platform configuration

This section teaches future operation. No deployment, configuration or data changes were performed for this investigation.

### Required: first owner and safe access

1. Have the deployment operator prepare a private/restricted initial installation. Follow [Self-hosting](docs/SELF_HOSTING.md) for persistent storage, public URL, HTTPS, secrets and the selected release. These are outside the application settings screen.
2. Open `/setup/` before exposing an uninitialized installation publicly. Enter **Username**, **Email**, password and password confirmation; select **Create Owner and Finish Setup**. This creates and signs in the first Platform Owner. There is no default administrator password.
3. On an initialized installation, Platform Owners sign in through `/config/login/`; normal accounts use `/accounts/login/`. The two forms deliberately reject the other account type.
4. Open your username menu → **Platform Administration**. Initial setup is no longer offered after an owner exists.
5. Use **Platform Owners** for additional equal-authority owners. Select **Generate Invitation**, then generate the invitation after confirming your current password; share the generated link privately. The invited person accepts it to create the owner account. Invitations are single-use and expire after seven days; **Revoke** cancels an unused valid invitation. This is not a Moderator invitation or a normal Reader account.

### Required: review community access policies

Open **Platform Administration → Settings → General Settings** (also a direct General Settings card on the administration home).

| Exact field | Purpose / initial model default | Operational choice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Platform Display Name** | Installation's human-readable name; `My Northbound` | Use the community name if desired. Application branding remains Northbound. |
| **Platform Timezone** | Installation-wide timestamps/backups/audit; deployment TIME_ZONE default | Set deliberately. This does not change Group timezones. |
| **Allow Public Registration** | New public account creation; initially enabled | Enable if members will create their own accounts. Disabling does not deactivate existing accounts. |
| **Allow Normal Accounts to Create Groups** | Self-service Group creation; initially enabled | Usually disable after the community Group is prepared if members should only join it. Owners can still create Groups. |

Choose **Save General Settings**. Runtime settings do not let you change ports, secret keys, database credentials, filesystem locations, proxy trust or container settings.

### Required: account and Group preparation

Create a normal account for the community organizer through **Create an Account**, or use the supported staff account-creation path after a Group exists. Normal account creation requires an email and password. As a normal account, **Home → Create Group** creates the first Group and makes that account its Group Owner. Configure the timezone and invite trusted staff before creating a Challenge.

If a Platform Owner creates a Group from the platform directory, that does **not** make the owner a member. Arrange a normal account's membership and Group Owner role explicitly; do not assume the platform account can appear in the Host selector.

Platform **Accounts** lists normal accounts. Open an account for **Edit Account**, **Reset Password**, **Deactivate** or **Reactivate**. Password reset supplies a temporary password; communicate it privately and have the Reader change it. There is no implemented public “Forgot password” email-reset route in the URL configuration. Account recovery email is useful identification, not proof that an email-reset service exists.

### Recommended and optional preparation

- **Recommended:** Settings → **Backups**, schedule SQLite backups and download a separate protected copy. Preserve encryption secrets separately. See section 13.
- **Recommended:** **System Status**, verify the deployed storage/database and diagnostics; `/health/` only proves database health.
- **Optional:** configure Group Hardcover catalog access and installation Reader OAuth, section 12. Neither is required for manual submissions.
- **Recommended:** agree staff responsibilities, review timing, team rules, optional bonuses and member-facing instructions before opening registration.

## 4. Group management

### Create and configure a Group

**Role:** normal account when Group creation is permitted; Platform Owner override also exists.

1. On Home select **Create Group**.
2. Enter **Name** and **Timezone**. New Groups default to `America/New_York`; choose the actual community timezone.
3. Optionally select **Enable Group Announcement** and supply **Group Announcement**. Enabled announcements require text.
4. Leave **Hardcover API Token** blank for a manual-only start, or supply the Group-owned catalog key and **Test Token**.
5. Select **Create Group**. A normal creator becomes Group Owner and a six-character access code is generated.
6. Later open the Group → **Group Settings**, adjust details and choose **Save**. Use **Edit Group Announcement** for that specific task.

A Challenge has its own announcement control. A Group announcement and an explicitly configured Challenge message are separate communication settings; see section 5 for the saved Challenge default and editor behavior.

### Invite existing accounts

**Role:** Group settings authority to manage codes; viewing depends on code visibility.

1. Open Group → **Access Code**, read **Current Code**, and share it privately.
2. The Reader selects Home → **Join With Code**, enters **Access Code**, then **Save**.
3. The person becomes a Group Member, not automatically a Challenge Reader.
4. **Access Code Visibility** offers **Group owners only** (default), **Owners and moderators**, or **All group members**. Choose **Save Settings** after changing it.
5. **Regenerate Code** immediately invalidates the old code for future joining. Existing memberships remain.

No join-request approval queue is implemented. An invalid code or inactive Group prevents joining.

### Create an account for a new member

**Role:** permanent-member management capability.

Open **Members → Add Member**, enter **Username**, **Display Name**, optional email, **Role**, and **Temporary Password** (at least 12 characters), then **Save**. Share credentials privately; the person signs in and changes their password. For an existing account, use the access code rather than creating a duplicate account.

### Roles, delegation and ownership

**Role:** Group permission-management capability.

Open **Members → Permissions** for the member. Select **Persistent Group Role**, review **Delegated Group Permissions**, then **Save Group Permissions**. Changes apply to that Group. Choose explicit Allow/Deny only when needed; otherwise use the role default.

There can be multiple Group Owners. Giving another member the Owner role shares ownership; there is no normal ownership-transfer wizard. You cannot change your own permissions/role on this screen. Have the other authorized Owner complete a handoff if your own role needs changing.

### Profiles and removals

Open **Members**, select a name for the Member Profile, and inspect the available reading summary. Another member's full Monthly History is not made available simply because you own the Group. Profile Discord visibility follows the Reader's **Make Discord username public** preference; operational staff access is a separate matter.

**Members → Remove** requires member-management authority and confirmation. It deactivates membership, preserving account, submissions and history; it is not a hard delete. You cannot remove yourself. A valid code permits rejoining. Review Challenge staffing/participation separately; do not treat Group removal as erasing Challenge history.

### Create a Group Challenge

With Challenge-management authority, open Group → **Create Challenge**, enter **Challenge Title**, use **Host → Add Host**, check **Selected Hosts**, then **Save**. At least one active normal Group member must be selected. Include yourself if you will operate teams and activities. Continue with the settings inventory below.

## 5. Challenge creation and every setting

There is no Standard/BOTM/TBR “format” dropdown. Create one Challenge, then enable optional features. New Challenges have a title and selected Hosts, start **Draft**, have registration closed, and have no schedule until configured.

In this section **manager** means Group Challenge-management capability or Platform Owner; **operator** means active Host or Platform Owner. Fields are editable later subject to the specific locks below. For the first community event, prefer simple rules: manual final completion, visible standings if wanted, zero bonuses until explained, and a fixed checkpoint if everyone has no history.

### General, schedule and announcement

| Screen → exact label | Default / options | Effect, later editing and authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create Challenge → **Challenge Title** | Required; at most 80 characters, unique within Group | Name members see. Manager creates; manager/operator later edits under General. |
| Create Challenge → **Hosts** | One or more required active normal Group members | Selected through Host picker / Add Host. Does not enroll them. Later Hosts assignment requires manager authority. |
| General → **Challenge Title**, **Description** | Title from creation; description blank | Explain event rules; **Save General Settings**. Manager/operator. General view has no normal lifecycle setup lock. |
| Challenge Schedule → **Registration Opens** | Initially unset | Group-local date/time; required on first schedule configuration. Automatic opening requires its checkbox. Manager/operator; **Save Schedule**. |
| **Open registration automatically** | On | At/after opening time opens registration in Draft/Upcoming/Active; also publishes Draft to Upcoming. |
| **Registration Closes** | Initially unset; cannot precede opening | Independent gate for new self-registration. Required when first configuring. |
| **Close registration automatically** | On | Closes registration when due, independently of lifecycle. |
| **Challenge Starts** | Initially unset; required on first configuration | Supplies Challenge start/date range. Does not activate without its checkbox or manual lifecycle action. |
| **Move Challenge to Active automatically** | On | Upcoming → Active when due. |
| **Challenge Ends** | Initially unset; cannot precede start; required initially | Supplies completion-date range and scheduled submission cutoff. |
| **Move Challenge to Finalizing automatically** | On | Active → Finalizing when due; stops new submissions, leaves review available. |

Schedule inputs use the Group timezone, not the Platform Timezone. Schedule editing is available beyond setup, but Archived schedule fields are disabled. Changing dates changes relevant date validation/reporting; it does not erase history or automatically reverse lifecycle. Avoid shifting a live event's dates casually. Automatic lifecycle processing advances only one adjacent stage per pass; entering several past timestamps is not one immediate leap to Completed.

### Registration and Personal TBR

Open **Challenge Settings → Signup Questions**.

| Exact label / control | Default / allowed values | Effect and edit restrictions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reader Answer Editing** | **Editing allowed for a set period**, default 24 hours; also **No editing after registration**, **Editing allowed until registration closes** | Manager/operator sets policy before first enrollment. Lock includes staff-created enrollment, not only answered questionnaires. |
| **Editing Duration (Hours)** | 24; 1–720 | Used only for timed editing; measured from original enrollment time. Rejoining does not reset it. |
| **Question** | Up to 240 characters | Up to ten custom questions total. **Add Question**, **Move Up**, **Move Down**, **Remove Question** control the builder. |
| **Type** | **Short Text**, **Number**, **Single Choice**, **Multiple Choice** | Determines answer control and validation. |
| **Required** | Optional unless checked | Reader must answer required questions. |
| **Choices** | One per line, 2–20 unique nonblank choices for choice types; each at most 120 characters | Not applicable to text/number questions. |
| **Enable Personal TBR** | Off | Readers may optionally register up to nine books. |
| **Per-book TBR Bonus Pages** | 0; nonnegative integer | Bonus per qualifying locked-list completion. |
| **Full 9-Book TBR Completion Bonus Pages** | 0; nonnegative integer | Extra reward only for exactly nine registered books and all nine qualifying completions. |

Choose **Save Registration Settings** for questions/policy. These controls lock once any enrollment exists, and outside Draft/Upcoming/Active. This can happen when a Host adds the first participant: finish signup design first.

Personal TBR has its own **Save Personal TBR Settings**. Manager/operator can change these feature settings in Draft/Upcoming/Active even after the question schema locks; saving reconciles relevant rewards. Reader lists themselves stay locked on confirmation. Previously established completion awards retain their recorded amount/set; do not assume a changed advertised amount rewrites earned awards.

The remaining configurable control is **Challenge Lifecycle**, described next. There is no automatic team balancing, registration approval switch, mandatory team setting, Reader submission-edit policy, general goal editor or separate “complete-all required to participate” switch.

## 6. Challenge lifecycle

Implemented sequence: **Draft → Upcoming → Active → Finalizing → Completed → Archived**. **Closed is not a lifecycle state.** Registration can separately be Open or Closed.

| State | How reached | Reader actions | Staff work and exit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Draft** | New Challenge | Normally hidden; no self-registration/submission | Prepare settings, questions, teams, activities and staff. Manual next stage or automatic registration opening publishes Upcoming. |
| **Upcoming** | Manual forward action or scheduled registration opening from Draft | Register only if registration gate open; no submissions; eligible withdrawal | Finish preparation and placements. Manual/scheduled start moves Active. |
| **Active** | Manual/scheduled start from Upcoming | Active Readers submit; new registration only while gate open; eligible withdrawal | Setup/roster work and review; scheduled/manual ending moves Finalizing. |
| **Finalizing** | Manual/scheduled end from Active | No new registration or book submission | Normal review continues; Games reward operations can continue under their rules. Most setup/roster tools lock. Manually complete after review, or automatic completion when configured. |
| **Completed** | Manual forward action or scheduled final announcement from Finalizing | History/reference; no ordinary submissions or review | Final results. Can advance to Archived. Returning to Finalizing needs explicit recovery confirmation. |
| **Archived** | Manual next stage from Completed | Historical reference where accessible; records retained | No normal backward lifecycle action. Not a delete. |

Manager/operator navigates **Challenge Settings → Challenge Lifecycle → Move Forward to [next stage]** and completes the displayed action. Only adjacent stages are allowed. **Move Back to [previous stage]** requires explicit confirmation; Completed → Finalizing additionally requires **Confirm Recovery to Finalizing**. Review automation before reversing, or it may advance again.

General, schedule, competition visibility and completion-match tools have their own access/edit rules; “read-only” messages on locked operations are not a claim that every installation or recovery control disappears. Archived is not an absolute prohibition on all Platform Owner recovery. Book/Theme review is specifically Active/Finalizing; ordinary setup and soft-removal are specifically Draft/Upcoming/Active.

Schedules require the deployed scheduler to run. The container normally includes it. Its source service processes due actions and checkpoints; it does not invent past checkpoint scores. A healthy website does not prove that the deployment's background scheduler is functioning.

## 7. Registration

### Prepare and open

**Role:** manager/operator. Configure questions and answer policy before **any** participation is added. Set registration timestamps and their automatic-action checkboxes under **Challenge Schedule → Save Schedule**. Publish/open through the schedule; there is no separate manual Open Registration button on the current settings screen. Moving Draft to Upcoming alone does not open registration.

Self-registration requires an active normal Group membership, Upcoming/Active lifecycle, open registration, and no conflicting active Floater role. There is no staff approval queue for successful registration. Staff may add eligible participants separately.

### Reader confirmation

The Reader opens the Challenge → **Register**, answers required questions, optionally provides **Discord Username (Optional)** if not already stored, prepares an optional TBR, selects **Review Registration**, checks **Registration Summary**, then **Confirm Registration**. **Back to Edit** returns before confirmation. Zero-question registration still requires confirmation.

Confirmation activates participation, saves answers, and locks the TBR (including an empty list). It does not assign a team. Discord capture does not switch the Reader's public-username preference on.

### Changes and staff access

- Reader: **Edit Registration → Save Registration** while allowed by the original editing policy. Timed windows follow enrollment time; the until-close option follows the gate. Do not assume a timed window ends exactly when new registration closes.
- Host/authorized Group Challenge manager: **Challenge Participants**, open registration details to read answers. Team Leaders/Floaters alone do not receive answer access. Hosts cannot rewrite someone else's answers.
- Platform Owner: registration detail additionally provides administrative answer correction through **Save Administrative Correction**, saved with audit evidence. It does not change enrollment dates, roles, teams or locked TBR.
- Host: **Challenge Participants → Add Participant**, select member and optional team → **Save**. This creates active participation, not fabricated answers or a confirmed TBR. Staff enrollment can lock signup configuration.
- Host: **Edit Team** changes placement; **Remove** ends participation and current team/leadership while retaining history. **Reactivate** restores participation, not old team/leadership. Normal roster changes require Draft/Upcoming/Active.
- Reader: **Withdraw** in Upcoming/Active, even if registration closed. Withdrawal preserves history and locks. If registration later permits, self-withdrawn Readers can re-register; staff-removed Readers need Host reactivation.

Registration closure stops new self-registration; it does not itself end Active submissions. When a Reader cannot register, check membership, lifecycle, gate, Floater conflict and staff removal before assuming a broken form.

## 8. Teams

**Role:** operator for team/roster administration; appointed Team Leaders review rather than manage teams.

1. Challenge → **Teams → Add Team**, enter **Name** and **Color**, then **Save**.
2. **Challenge Participants → Edit Team**, choose placement → **Save**. Existing Group membership and active eligible participation are required; adding a participant with a team can establish participation.
3. Open team → **Manage Team Leaders**, choose an eligible current Reader → **Assign Team Leader**.
4. Check **Current Roster**. Leaders appear first, then other Readers; default sorting is alphabetical within those groups.
5. To move someone, change **Edit Team**. Old assignment remains in history; old-team leadership ends. Assign new-team leadership explicitly if needed.
6. To end leadership use **Remove Team Leader**; inspect **Team Leader History** as needed.

Readers may participate without a team. Unassigned does not mean Floater: a Floater is non-competing review staff and cannot simultaneously be an active Reader/team member in that Challenge.

**Edit Team → Save Team** changes name/color. **Archive Team** removes it from active selectors/views while preserving history. **View Archive → Restore Team** brings it back. **Delete Team** is restricted to unused teams in Draft; do not use deletion to reorganize historical teams.

Team scores aggregate the current active roster's Challenge scores. They are not a permanent allocation of each book to the team a Reader belonged to on the completion date. Moving Readers can therefore change team totals. Games rewards retain their original recipients; moving someone does not award the old reward to their replacement.

**Teams**, team detail and **Team Comparison** provide competition reporting subject to visibility. Reports distinguish **Base**, **Modifier**, **Total**. In team detail, check **From**, **To**, **Show Bonuses**, available **Show Readers** feature filters and planning columns. Select **Apply Filters**, or **Reset Filters** before comparing screens. **Displayed Team Total** follows filters; **Current Readers** remains a roster count. TBR progress uses the actual registered list size, even though the full TBR bonus needs nine.

Open a Reader's Participant Detail with self, scoped review or TBR-management authority. Depending on permissions it combines **Score**, **Planning / History**, **Registration**, Personal TBR, BOTM, Themes, **Submissions**, and **Modifiers / Rewards**. Registration answers remain more restricted than ordinary review.

## 9. Challenge formats and activities

These features can coexist in one Challenge. They do not replace normal completed-book submission.

### A. Standard reading Challenge

**Setup:** manager creates Challenge/Hosts and schedule; operator organizes teams; Readers register. Leave BOTM, TBR and Games off for the simplest event. No custom questions or Themes are mandatory.

**Participation:** active Readers submit completed books while Active. Manual books wait for review; valid catalog selections can approve automatically. Approved pages are base progress. No automatic minimum number of books is required to complete the Challenge's lifecycle.

**Scoring example:** two approved books of 250 and 300 pages give 550 Base and 550 Total without rewards. A pending third book contributes zero until approved. Staff finish review in Finalizing and complete the event deliberately.

### B. Book of the Month (BOTM)

**Role:** operator for setup and match decisions. Reader for participation.

1. **Challenge Settings → BOTM Settings**: check **Enable Book of the Month**, set **Full BOTM Completion Bonus Pages**, then **Save Settings**.
2. **Books → Add Book**: use **Find on Hardcover → Find Book**, or **Clear and Enter Manually**. Set Position (1–9), Title, Author, Page Count and Per-book Bonus Pages → **Save Book**.
3. Explain the active set (one to nine books) and rewards to Readers. They open **Book of the Month**, read a selected book, and submit it normally.
4. An approved eligible submission is matched by book identity or deterministic normalized title/author where applicable. Ambiguity can require a separate match decision. Page approval is not necessarily a completed BOTM match.
5. Open **Book of the Month → Match History**. Inspect evidence; **Confirm** or **Reject** the candidate if a decision is needed. **Synchronize** rechecks Northbound matches; it does not synchronize Hardcover libraries.
6. Check Reader completion displays and **Completion Awards**, distinguishing **Currently qualifying** from historical entries.

Each qualifying book earns its configured per-book bonus once under its matching rules. Completing the entire configured set can earn the additional full-set bonus; completing just one of a three-book set does not earn that extra reward. There is no separate “must read all to submit” switch.

Example: three listed books, each 20 bonus pages, plus 100 for all three. A Reader completing two gets approved base pages +40; completing the third adds its base pages +20 and the 100 completion reward. Bonus zero still allows logical completion tracking.

**Edit**, **Retire**, **Reactivate** and **Delete** manage the list in Draft/Upcoming/Active; retirement preserves history, reactivation needs an available position, and deletion is blocked when history requires preservation. Later list/amount edits are not a simple rewrite of historical completion awards: the awarded set and amount are retained. Disabling preserves books but changes current feature qualification; inspect active reward history after changes. Agree a stable list before inviting Readers.

### C. Personal TBR

**Role:** manager/operator for feature settings; Readers choose their lists, with scoped staff assistance.

1. **Challenge Settings → Signup Questions → Personal TBR**: enable it, set **Per-book TBR Bonus Pages** and **Full 9-Book TBR Completion Bonus Pages** → **Save Personal TBR Settings**.
2. During registration a Reader enters up to nine planned books, using their personal Hardcover connection or manual Title/Author (Page Count optional).
3. They organize entries with **Add Book**, **Move Up**, **Move Down**, **Remove**, review warnings, and **Confirm Registration**.
4. The confirmed list locks, even if empty. Answer-editing permissions and re-registering do not unlock it. Authorized staff can make protected corrections through **Manage TBR**.
5. Reader submits finished books normally and views **Personal TBR** progress.
6. Authorized reviewers open **Review Queue → Personal TBR Matches**. Inspect candidate evidence, **Confirm**/**Reject** within review scope; **Synchronize** rechecks local matching.

Approved, qualifying matched books earn per-book rewards. A shorter list can earn those but **never** the full nine-book bonus. The full reward requires exactly nine registered books and completion of all nine. Repeated submissions cannot earn unlimited copies of a list-book reward; matching eligibility and durable history prevent duplicate qualification.

Example: four registered books with a 25-page per-book reward give up to 100 modifier pages for those four, plus base reading; a configured 200-page full-list reward still does not apply. With nine registered and all nine qualifying, 9 × 25 + 200 = 425 modifier pages. An empty confirmed list earns no TBR rewards.

Only pending matches can be adjudicated; Confirm/Reject is not a normal editor for finalized decisions. The BOTM/TBR match history, synchronization and adjudication implementation does not impose the same explicit lifecycle gate as book/Theme review. Eligibility, pending status, staffing and existing qualifying-match checks still apply. Do not assume Completed/Archived makes these specific operations unavailable or use synchronization casually after announcing final scores. The TBR link from Review Queue is normally reached during Active/Finalizing because that queue itself is stage-limited.

### Help a Reader manage their Challenge TBR

When TBR is enabled, open **Challenge Participants → Manage TBR**, or choose
**Manage TBR** on the Reader's Participant Detail. The Reader must already have
a Challenge enrollment. Group Owners and Moderators can help participants in
their Group without a Host assignment. Hosts and Floaters can help throughout
their assigned Challenge; Team Leaders can help only Readers currently assigned
to their own team. This permission does not grant access to registration answers,
account settings or the Reader's Hardcover credentials.

Use **Add Book**, **Move Up**, **Move Down** and **Remove** to organize up to nine
books. Search Hardcover to add or replace a selection, or enter **Title** and
**Author** manually; **Page Count (Optional)** may be blank.

- **Save TBR** saves the current selections without finalizing an incomplete list.
- **Save & Complete TBR** finalizes an incomplete list only when all nine selections
  are valid. It is not shown once the TBR is complete. This completes the planned
  list, not the reading of those books or the full-list reading bonus.

Neither action completes Challenge registration, supplies required answers,
reactivates participation or bypasses registration requirements. Readers still
submit finished books through the usual submission and review process.

Staff can correct selections after the normal Reader editing lock. Books with
match or award history must retain their selection and position; conflicting
changes are blocked with an explanation. Completed submissions, approved pages,
earned bonuses and historical checkpoint snapshots are preserved. The editor
shows who last updated the list and whether the change was staff-assisted or
Reader-managed.

Search first uses the acting staff member's authorized personal Hardcover
connection, then an available authorized Group catalog connection. Manual entry
remains available when neither works. The Reader need not connect Hardcover,
and staff never use or see that Reader's private credentials. Search connections
are read-only for this feature: saving or completing a TBR never queues reading
sync or writes reading activity to staff or Group Hardcover accounts. The Reader's
own connection, consent and forward-only synchronization remain separate.

### Themes and Games

New Themes use **Theme Bonus (%)**: a whole percentage from 0–100, default 0%.
The bonus uses the book's **Approved Pages**, excluding bonuses. Each Theme is
rounded separately to the nearest whole page, with half pages rounded up:
400 × 25% = 100; 333 × 25% = 83; 334 × 25% = 84; 1 × 25% = 0.
Stacked Themes use the same base independently and their rounded awards are added;
percentages never compound. The rate locks as soon as any Theme claim exists,
including pending or rejected claims; other settings retain their existing editing rules.
Existing Themes remain clearly labeled **legacy fixed-page** rules; their values
are not converted. Approval records the percentage, approved-page basis and awarded
pages, including a finalized zero. Rejection/reapproval and removal/restoration
preserve that evidence. An authorized **Approved Pages** correction during normal
review recalculates percentage awards transactionally, without changing fixed-page
awards, unrelated rewards or historical checkpoint snapshots. Corrections also update established rejected-claim calculations without changing
the claim status or activating rewards. Authorized restoration initializes missing
first-time awards once the book becomes eligible. Ordinary reports,
catalog changes and repeat reviews do not recalculate awards. Completed/Archived
Challenges cannot receive ordinary review corrections; existing recovery safeguards
remain in force. There is no conversion control or live calculation preview.


**Themes:** operator opens **Themes → Add Theme**, enters the fields in section 5 and **Save**. Readers select **Theme Claims** during submission and answer each selected prompt. Completed On must be in the Theme's inclusive window. A non-stacking Theme cannot be selected with another Theme on the same book. Both approved book and approved claim are needed for its bonus. Prior awarded amounts are preserved rather than mass-edited when advertised bonuses change.

**Games:** operator enables Games, creates an activity through **Games → Add Game**, sets Name, Starts At, Ends At and Maximum Bonus Pages, and saves. Readers see activities; Hosts deliberately award pages. No automatic award follows from the Game's dates.

During Active/Finalizing, for an enabled active Game, open **Apply Reward**, enter **Amount**, **Apply To** (**Individual**, **Team**, **Challenge-wide**), choose Reader/team where needed, and enter **Reason / Note**. Review **Confirm Reward** including recipients, then **Confirm and Apply Reward**. Amount applies **to each recipient**, not as a divided pool. Each application is capped by Maximum Bonus Pages. The effective date is the Group-local application date; recipients are frozen at application time.

Use **Edit Game → Save Game** for configuration changes in Draft/Upcoming/Active. **Retire Game** prevents future reward applications while preserving past rewards/scores; **Reactivate Game** allows future applications again. **Delete Unused Game** is for a Game without reward history; read its confirmation. These setup/status operations lock after Active. Changing the cap does not rewrite a frozen earlier reward application.

**Reward History → Void Reward** removes the scoring effect and retains history/reason. A reward is a modifier, not a submitted book or base pages. Disabling Games preserves existing rewards/scores. Use reward history to explain corrections; do not add arbitrary approved pages to a book to imitate a Game reward.

## 10. Reading submissions

### Reader workflow

**Required:** active normal Group membership and active enrollment; Challenge Active. No team is required.

1. Open Challenge → **Submit Book**.
2. If Group catalog access is available, **Find on Hardcover → Title, Author, or ISBN → Search**, or **Hardcover Book or Edition Link → Import Link**.
3. Select the correct result and edition. The selected edition supplies identity/format and a usable scoring edition supplies pages. Audio can use an ebook/print page equivalent. No usable scoring pages means continue manually with a reference.
4. Review **Book Details**: **Title**, **Author**, **Book Format**, **Started On** (optional), **Completed On**, **Submitted Pages**, **Reference Link** (optional), **Notes**, and optional **Theme Claims**/prompt responses.
5. Use **Clear and Enter Manually** when needed; enter the actual edition's details/pages, not bonuses. Formats are Paperback, Hardcover, E-book, Audio, Manga, Fanfic, Other.
6. **Submit Book** returns to the Challenge. Usable catalog selections are verified/Approved automatically; manual entries are Pending review. Theme claims remain separate.

Submitted Pages must be positive; completion must fall inside the Challenge date range; Started On cannot follow Completed On. Starting before the Challenge is not itself forbidden by that validation. A catalog selection can expire after a day: select again rather than assuming it stays valid indefinitely.

### Staff review

**Role:** Platform Owner or current Host/Floater/Team Leader in scope. **Stage:** Active or Finalizing.

1. Open **Needs Attention → Open Review Queue**, or Challenge → **Review Queue**.
2. Open a book. Inspect **Submitted Book**, Submitted Pages, format, dates, link and Reader Notes.
3. For manual books, in **Decision** set **Approved Pages**, **Status**, optional **Verification Link**, and **Review Notes**. Approved requires a positive approved count.
4. Decide each **Theme Claims** status separately; approving the book does not approve all its claims.
5. Choose **Save Review**. Recheck Base/Modifier/Total and match progress when relevant.

Rejected books contribute no base pages; rejection also rejects their Theme claims. Correcting a manual review decision during the review stages is possible by reopening it from Participant Detail → Submissions. Catalog-verified books have locked approved pages and approval status in normal review; use Theme review independently. Do not promise reviewers an override for those pages.

### Corrections, removal and history

Readers have **no normal edit/delete/resubmit action** for an existing entry. Staff review changes decisions, not title, author or reading dates. Tell Readers to report the specific mistake to a Host rather than submit an extra copy.

For an entry that should not count, an operator can use the Challenge submission list's **Remove** during Draft/Upcoming/Active, read the confirmation, supply the requested reason, and confirm. It is soft-removal: the record/removal attribution persists while active scoring excludes it. It is not available in Finalizing just because review is available. If a replacement entry is appropriate, agree it with the Host and remove the wrong record while normal controls permit; do not promise an in-place metadata editor.

There is no general submission uniqueness constraint preventing every repeated book. A reread and an accidental duplicate require staff judgment; feature-match constraints are not a universal duplicate-submission detector. Inspect the Reader's history before approving suspicious duplicates. Recovery, where necessary, belongs to Platform Owner tools, not a fictitious Reader correction button.

**My Stats → My Book History** gives personal book/status history; Participant Detail gives scoped operational history; **Audit Activity** records administration/review and **Recovery History** records exceptional recovery. “Void” is a normal Games reward operation; submissions use Remove/restore recovery rather than a normal Void Submission button.

## 11. Reading targets and checkpoints

### Previous monthly average

Planning uses this Reader's eligible **Completed** Challenges in the same Group, excluding the current Challenge. Each contributes approved non-removed **base** pages; bonuses do not inflate it. Participation withdrawn by the Reader is excluded. A qualifying participated Challenge with zero approved pages contributes zero. No qualifying history displays N/A, not zero. Archived Challenges are not included by the current Completed-state query, so archiving older events can change later planning averages.

Example: two eligible Challenges with 400 and 600 approved base pages give Avg Pages = 500 and Last Challenge = 600. A 100-page Game bonus in either does not raise this baseline. Staff-added participation is not automatically evidence that custom registration was completed.

### Configure checkpoints

**Role:** manager/operator. Open **Challenge Settings → Progress Checkpoints → Add Checkpoint**. Set date/time, threshold percentage, progress basis, target basis and fixed target when needed. Order/remove entries as appropriate → **Save Checkpoints**. At most five; configuration locks once any has evaluated/skipped, and after Active.

Required progress = target × threshold / 100. A **Fixed Target** of 600 with 25% threshold requires 150 pages. At a **Base Pages** checkpoint, 120 base +50 bonuses is below target; **Total Pages** would measure 170 and meet it. With Previous Monthly Average 500 and 50%, required pages are 250. Missing previous history produces Not evaluated, not a failure. A zero historical target can be met with zero.

The scheduler evaluates active Readers when a checkpoint becomes due during Active. It snapshots the actual totals available at evaluation time, not a reconstruction of reading as it looked at the nominal timestamp. Late processing therefore matters. After Finalizing/Completed/Archived it skips an overdue pending checkpoint instead of manufacturing historical results. Later approvals, removals and rewards do not rewrite captured results.

Below-target warnings are staff-facing, including Host participant planning and Needs Attention. They do not remove Readers, deduct pages, block submissions or automatically rebalance teams. There is no Reader checkpoint dashboard or independent personal-goal setup wizard. Explain targets to members in announcements and use staff follow-up supportively.

Team progress is aggregation/reporting of current Readers and their feature progress; checkpoints evaluate individuals, not a separate configurable team target. Compare the same date range, base/total basis and roster before calling a team total wrong.

## 12. Hardcover integration

This section describes Northbound's implemented integration contract. Live provider screens, token issuance, OAuth and external writes were not exercised; confirm those on an explicitly authorized non-production deployment before launch.

### A. Platform Administrator

**Deployment preparation:** the operator configures the intended public `NORTHBOUND_URL`, HTTPS and persistent secrets as described in [Self-hosting](docs/SELF_HOSTING.md). Configure and preserve `NORTHBOUND_TOKEN_ENCRYPTION_KEY` before saving credentials. Existing installations without it use a key derived from the original Django secret; changing the active key makes saved credentials unreadable. Do not put credentials in training examples, logs or Discord.

**Reader OAuth:** Platform Owner opens **Platform Administration → Settings → Hardcover OAuth**.

1. Create this installation's own Hardcover Developer App using the Northbound page's supplied values. The application expects **Web app (server-side)**, confidential client/secret and PKCE; Device Authorization Grant off.
2. Copy displayed **Website URL** and **Redirect URI** exactly. They derive from configured NORTHBOUND_URL, not the address used to open the page. Callback ends `/account/hardcover/oauth/callback/`, including trailing slash. Public OAuth requires HTTPS; loopback development is the exception.
3. Allow `read:catalog`, `read:library`, `write:library`. Broader account/review/catalog editing scopes are unnecessary.
4. Enter **Client ID**, **Client Secret**, check **Enable Hardcover OAuth**, then **Save Configuration**. Secret is encrypted and not redisplayed. Blank on later edits preserves the saved secret.
5. Check **Configured**, then have Readers connect themselves. Configured is a local configuration/decryption result, not a live provider test.

Changing public origin requires the Developer App URLs to match. A Client ID change requires Readers to reconnect. OAuth being disabled does not disable scoped personal API keys or Group keys. Platform Owners cannot impersonate Reader connections or turn their consent on.

**Group catalog connection:** Group settings/integration authority opens Group → **Group Settings** → **Connect Hardcover** (or **Replace Hardcover Token**), enters **API Token**, uses **Test Token** or **Save and Test**, then checks **Working**, **Needs Attention**, and **Last Tested**. Use Group-owned scopes `read:catalog:data` and `read:catalog:search`. **Test Connection** rechecks saved access. **Disconnect → Confirm Disconnect** stops it. Hosts/Moderators do not gain this authority merely from their titles.

Group catalog powers submission/BOTM search. Reader credentials never substitute for it; Group keys never substitute for private Reader access. Group OAuth is not implemented in this version.

### B. Individual Reader

**My Account → Personal Hardcover Connection → Connect with Hardcover** starts OAuth. Reader approves the provider request and returns; check Connected and granted permissions. Existing connections may offer **Reconnect / Update Permissions** or **Switch to OAuth**. A replacement must succeed before replacing working access.

Alternatively expand **Advanced: scoped API key**, enter **Personal Hardcover API Token**, then **Connect with Scoped API Key**. Use the Reader's own key with `read:catalog`, `read:library`, `write:library`. Existing keys may show **Replace Scoped API Key** or **Switch to Scoped API Key**. **Test Saved Connection** checks access; PAT catalog testing cannot prove all scopes/expiry or library writes.

Both **Hardcover Synchronization** preferences start OFF:

- **Sync completed books:** new eligible approvals can create a missing personal library entry or mark an existing entry Read.
- **Sync Northbound completion dates:** additionally sends the actual finish date as a read occurrence; requires completed-book consent.

Select preferences → **Save Synchronization Preferences**. Connection alone enables no export. Personal TBR search can work without export consent.

### What is automatic, manual or unsupported

| Behavior | What actually happens |
| --- | --- |
| Shared search / edition lookup | Reader/Host explicitly searches using the Group key; no personal connection required. |
| Personal TBR search | Reader explicitly searches using their own connection; manual entry is also available. |
| Eligible future approved book after consent | Queued for background processing; safe Hardcover identity and valid personal write access needed. |
| Already-Read library book | Not duplicated as another library entry. Date-consented distinct rereads may have distinct read occurrences. |
| Date corrections | Where safely mapped, target the saved occurrence, not unrelated same-book history. This is not a normal Reader metadata-edit feature. |
| OAuth renewal | Refresh happens when needed; failure/uncertain refresh can require reconnect. |
| Sync Now | Wakes existing eligible queued work; does not scan/queue old approvals or do a full immediate export. |
| Prior approved history, import from Hardcover | No historical backfill, bulk export or personal reading-history import. |
| Ratings, reviews, catalog edits, deletion writes | Not supported. A Northbound correction/disconnect does not promise to undo external history. |

Provider outages do not prevent Northbound approval/scoring. Synchronization health distinguishes **Pending**, **Retry scheduled**, **Reconnect required**, **Reconciliation required**, **Recovery review**, plus **Last success**/**Last safe issue** where available. Retryable errors are retried in the background; uncertain writes and exhausted/restored work are held rather than blindly repeated. Reconnecting and Sync Now do not bypass those holds; some unprovable external outcomes have no automatic resolver in v1.

**Disconnect** on the Reader account acts immediately without a second confirmation screen, clears saved personal access and turns both preferences OFF. Northbound submissions/TBR/history and already-sent external reading remain. OAuth revocation is best-effort; if needed the Reader also removes authorization at Hardcover. Reconnection does not reactivate consent after disconnect or backfill old books. Eligible unfinished queued work may resume when credentials and consent permit.

After restore, OAuth Readers must reconnect; follow the documented restored-sync safeguards. The database/encryption key alone cannot undo external writes or restore spent provider authorizations. See [Hardcover operations](docs/HARDCOVER.md) and [Backup and restore](docs/BACKUP_RESTORE.md).

### Backups and operational controls

Under Backups configure **Enable Automatic Backups**, **Backup Days**, **Backup Time**, **Backups to Keep** (1–100). Times use Platform Timezone. Choose **Save Backup Schedule**. **Create Stored Backup** creates a ZIP on the server; use **Download** separately to retain an off-volume copy. Built-in ZIPs include a consistent SQLite copy, uploaded media and backup metadata, but not deployment configuration or encryption keys. Follow [Backup and restore](docs/BACKUP_RESTORE.md) for the exact backup type/media coverage and restore procedure; do not assume every database-only backup includes uploaded media or encryption keys. Stored rows provide **Download**, **Restore**, **Delete**.

For an uploaded backup, select **Northbound Backup ZIP → Validate and Stage Restore**. After validation follow **Restart and Restore** when available, or the documented operator restart path. Restore replaces installation state and can require application restart. Use its impact/confirmation and restart instructions, a current separate backup, the original encryption key and restored-sync safeguards. PostgreSQL backup/restore is external, not an automatic extension of the SQLite UI. Do not practice restore on the community's live instance.

Storage & Maintenance offers **Review Cache Cleanup**, **Keep Audit History For → Review Audit Pruning**, and SQLite **Review Optimization**. These first show the relevant review/confirmation. Cache is disposable metadata; audit pruning removes eligible ordinary history; optimization returns unused SQLite space and can briefly need exclusive access. It is unnecessary after every deletion. PostgreSQL maintenance remains external.

### Recovery tools: operational boundaries

Open **Advanced Recovery**, select the domain, inspect the target and impact, then use only the displayed bounded action. Every offered action is subject to its own checks; a card is not permission to perform arbitrary edits.

| Domain | Supported purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Challenges & Participation** | Recover staffing, enrollment, teams/assignments; exceptional one-Challenge purge with dependency impact. |
| **Groups & Memberships** | Deactivate/reactivate, ownership recovery and membership state/role correction. |
| **Accounts** | State recovery preserving stable identity/history. |
| **Submission Recovery** | Restore/soft-remove or exceptionally purge an entry, reconciling score sources. |
| **Theme Recovery** | Retire/reactivate, correct unused configuration, reconcile claim decisions. |
| **Modifier Recovery** | Source-aware void/restore/rebuild or purge malformed Theme reward records; no arbitrary score-amount editor. |
| **BOTM Recovery** | Book/match/per-book reward and source-driven completion repair. |
| **Personal TBR Recovery** | Match repair or exceptional repair of a locked entry / explicit locked-list rebuild. Not a normal Host convenience. |
| **Game Reward Recovery** | Retire Games; void/restore/repair/replace frozen applications under safeguards. |
| **Checkpoint Recovery** | Whole-checkpoint reset under Recovery Hold, followed by explicit scheduler release. Not individual result edits. |
| **Hardcover Credential Recovery** | Clear a broken Group/Reader connection without showing or replacing credentials. Not consent impersonation. |

Tier 1 is routine confirmation/audit. Tier 2 includes required reason, explicit confirmation, impact and durable recovery evidence. Tier 3 adds exact typed confirmation, current Platform Owner password, backup advisory and operation ID. The page treats a backup within 24 hours as recent; its advisory does not itself block emergency recovery. Read the exact target/action text each time.

There is no generic recovery CRUD, Group/User hard purge, anonymization, arbitrary frozen reward editing, individual checkpoint-result editing or generic completion-award editing. The separate Django `/platform-admin/` route exists, but this guide's supported community workflows use Northbound's purpose-built screens; do not substitute raw model edits for their safeguards.

## 14. Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Checks and supported action |
| --- | --- |
| Reader cannot join Group | Confirm normal account sign-in, six-character current access code, active Group, and whether code was regenerated. Owner shares the correct current code. No join-request approval workflow exists. |
| Create Account unavailable | Platform Owner checks Allow Public Registration. Existing accounts still sign in; authorized staff can create a new member through the Group path. |
| Cannot create Group | Check Allow Normal Accounts to Create Groups. Owning another Group does not bypass the installation policy. |
| Reader cannot register | Check active membership, Upcoming/Active stage, separate Open gate and scheduler, existing participation, staff removal and Floater conflict. Staff-removed requires Host Reactivate. |
| Signup settings unexpectedly locked | Any enrollment, including staff-added participants, locks schema/edit policy. There is no ordinary unlock button. Design before enrollment. |
| Cannot edit answers / TBR | Original timed policy/gate controls answers; confirmed TBR stays locked. Rejoining does not reset either. Platform Owner answer correction is separate from locked-list recovery. |
| Reader cannot submit | Require Active state, configured Challenge dates, active membership/enrollment and no Floater conflict. Check completion-date range, Started On ordering, positive pages and Theme prompts/stacking. Team is not required. |
| Book missing from search | Check which connection owns that search: Group for submissions/BOTM, personal Reader for TBR. Try title/author/ISBN or supported Hardcover link. Use manual entry if catalog access/pages are unavailable. |
| Submission not counted | Pending/rejected/removed books contribute no approved base. Inspect status and approved count; check report filters. A pending Theme or ambiguous BOTM/TBR match is not an earned bonus. |
| Catalog pages cannot be changed | Normal review locks catalog verification. Do not enter a fabricated bonus into Approved Pages; escalate a wrong entry for supported removal/recovery. |
| Team score appears wrong | Check current roster, active enrollments, moved Readers, date filters, Show Bonuses and Show Readers. Compare Base/Modifier/Total. Game recipients remain fixed; displayed filtered total may differ from all-team comparison. |
| My Stats total differs from book rows | Summary **Verified Pages** includes modifiers, despite its label. Book-history rows use approved base, or submitted pages if unapproved. Do not add those unlike figures together. |
| Missing BOTM/TBR bonus | Check feature enabled, active eligible participation, approved non-removed book, correct book identity, match qualification, configured amount and full-set requirements. Inspect match history; do not repeatedly resubmit. |
| Checkpoint missing / surprising | Check scheduled time, Active at processing, baseline availability, base/total choice, target and original snapshot. No-history may be Not evaluated. Overdue post-Active checkpoints are skipped. |
| Challenge not advancing | Check exact stage, Group-local dates and respective automation boxes. Draft does not auto-start directly as Active; automatic publication needs registration-opening action. Each pass advances one stage. Operator reviews deployed scheduler/status; do not run a scheduler against production merely as a training experiment. |
| Hardcover fails | Check Group vs Reader ownership, saved connection status, OAuth public origin/callback/client configuration, encryption-key continuity and expiry/permission messages. Re-test/reconnect using supported controls. Never request a Reader's private token in Discord. |
| Sync Now changes nothing | It only wakes existing eligible work. Check consent was enabled before approval and inspect health/holds. No historical export or automatic ambiguous-write resolver exists. External sync failure does not invalidate Northbound scoring. |
| Reader cannot edit a submission | Expected: no normal edit action. Ask Host; manual review corrects decisions, not book metadata. Remove during permitted stages or escalate exceptional recovery. |
| Moderator cannot access administration | Group announcements are the only default extra Group capability. Check the exact delegated capability, active membership, assigned Challenge role, team review scope and lifecycle. Do not promote to Platform Owner just to provide book review. |
| Review Queue empty but bonus missing | Approved catalog book may have no pending page review; inspect Theme decisions and BOTM/TBR matches separately. Check reviewer scope and removed entries. |
| Cannot remove book during Finalizing | Review remains allowed; normal soft-removal does not. Coordinate Platform Owner recovery if justified rather than inventing an available Host action. |

Start with manual final completion, no surprise bonuses, an explicitly chosen visibility policy, and a fixed target or no checkpoints for a new Group with no history. ### Documentation discrepancies and easily misleading text

- Existing `docs/ROLES_AND_LIFECYCLE.md` describes Moderator defaults as including restricted team statistics. Current permission defaults grant only Group announcements; competition access requires the current visibility rules or a separate qualifying permission/assignment.
- `docs/HARDCOVER.md` loosely refers to Group Owner/Moderator/Platform Owner credential authority. The implementation specifically checks Group settings/integration capability; Moderator title alone is insufficient.
- Platform dashboard/Settings descriptions call Reader OAuth “future,” but the Reader OAuth routes, account controls and implementation exist in v1.0.1.
- Existing guides broadly describe later Challenges as read-only. Setup/roster/review locks differ: General lacks that ordinary setup lock, schedule disables Archived fields, visibility is setup-stage locked, completion-match tools have separate eligibility checks without an explicit lifecycle gate, and recovery is separate. - Requested “Closed” corresponds operationally to registration Closed or the lifecycle Finalizing, not a seventh Challenge state. Standard/BOTM/TBR are not mutually exclusive creation types.
