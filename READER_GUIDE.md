# Northbound — Reader's Guide

Theme guidance includes the percentage-bonus update; pre-existing Themes retain legacy fixed-page rules.

Welcome! Northbound is where your community organizes reading Challenges, collects completed books and keeps track of results. This guide covers v1.0.1. Your community's dates, optional activities and visibility settings determine which controls you see.

## 1. Welcome to Northbound

Think of your **Group** as your reading community's home. A **Challenge** is one reading event inside that Group, with its own dates, Readers, teams and activities. Joining a Group and joining a Challenge are separate steps.

You read a book, submit it during an Active Challenge, and follow its status. Approved reading contributes pages. Some Challenges also award bonus pages for Themes, Book of the Month, Personal TBR or Games.

Your Northbound history comes from Challenge participation. Northbound does not import your past Hardcover reading or provide a separate lifetime reading-log entry form. You do not need a Hardcover account to participate.

## 2. Getting started

### Create an account and sign in

1. Open the community's Northbound address.
2. On **Sign In**, select **Create an Account** if public registration is available.
3. Enter your username, email, password and confirmation. Optionally choose an avatar using **Built-In Avatar** or **Upload Your Own**.
4. Select **Create Account**. Successful registration signs you in and opens Home.
5. For later visits, use **Sign In** with your normal account. Platform Owner sign-in is a separate staff entrance.

If registration is unavailable, ask community staff how to obtain an account. If staff gave you a temporary password, sign in and change it. There is no public email-based Forgot Password workflow in this version; contact staff for account recovery. Keep passwords and Hardcover keys private.

### Update your account

Open your username menu → **My Account**. Review your name, email and optional avatar, then **Save Account**. Use **Change Password** for your password.

**Discord Username** is optional. Appropriate Group/Challenge staff can use it to organize participation. **Make Discord username public** starts off; enable it only if you want other Group members to see it on your Group profile. Providing it during registration does not automatically make it public. Your email is not shown in member rosters.

The username menu also provides **Home**, **My Stats**, **Light / Dark**, and **Sign Out**.

### Join your Group

1. Ask staff for the Group's current six-character code.
2. Home → **Join With Code**.
3. Enter **Access Code** → **Save**.
4. Home's **Your Reading Groups** now includes the Group. Open it to find visible Challenges.

A replaced code stops working; ask for the current one. Joining does not assign a team or register you for a Challenge. If **Create Group** is visible, that creates another community; use Join With Code to enter the existing one.

### Understand the dashboard

Home lists your active reading Groups. Open a Group, then a Challenge; check its announcement and **Challenge Schedule**. Draft Challenges are preparation spaces and are normally hidden from Readers. Upcoming does not always mean registration is already open.

## 3. Joining a Challenge

### Register

**You need:** an active normal membership in that Group; an Upcoming or Active Challenge with registration Open; no conflicting Floater assignment.

1. Open the Challenge → **Register**.
2. Answer its questions. Required questions must be completed; optional questions can be blank. You may see **Discord Username (Optional)** if it is not already stored.
3. If enabled, prepare your optional **Personal TBR** before continuing; see section 6.
4. Select **Review Registration**.
5. Read **Registration Summary**, including TBR warnings. Select **Back to Edit** if anything needs changing.
6. Select **Confirm Registration**.

You are now participating. Registration is not waiting for a separate approval step. A Host organizes team placement separately. You still confirm registration when there are no custom questions.

A Host can also add participation directly. That does not mean they answered registration questions or created a TBR for you. Ask the Host if your status is unclear rather than assuming a staff-added entry has all the same information as your own confirmed registration.

### Edit registration answers

When **Edit Registration** is available, change your answers → **Save Registration**. Each Challenge permits no editing, a limited number of hours from original enrollment, or editing until registration closes. Once your window ends, answers lock. A timed editing window is separate from the window for new registrations.

A confirmed TBR stays locked even if registration answers are still editable. Withdrawing and joining again does not reset either lock. Hosts can read answers for planning but cannot normally rewrite them for you; ask staff if an important correction is needed.

Answers are not public to other Readers. Hosts and authorized Group managers can view them. Team Leader/Floater status by itself does not expose your registration answers.

### Roles and your team

- **Reader:** participating in this Challenge; can submit completed books when Active.
- **Team Leader:** a Reader appointed to help review their current team's books and claims.
- **Host:** runs this Challenge. A Host who competes must also register as a Reader.
- **Floater:** non-competing review support, without a team or active Reader participation in that Challenge.

Open **Teams**, then your team for its roster. Ask a Host to fix placement or move you; Readers cannot move themselves. You can submit as an active Reader without a team. Unassigned does not mean Floater.

### Withdraw

In Upcoming/Active, **Withdraw** ends active participation and current team placement after confirmation; history remains. This can still be available after registration closes. If registration is open later, self-withdrawn Readers may re-register with existing locks preserved. If staff removed you, a Host must reactivate you. Previous team/leadership is not restored automatically.

## 4. Submitting books

**You need:** active Group membership, active Challenge participation, and the Challenge **Active**. Books must have a completion date inside the Challenge's configured date range.

### Search and select the right edition

1. Challenge → **Submit Book** opens **Submit a Completed Book**.
2. If shared search is connected, under **Find on Hardcover** enter **Title, Author, or ISBN** → **Search**. Alternatively enter **Hardcover Book or Edition Link** → **Import Link**.
3. Choose **View Editions** on your book result, then **Use Edition** for the edition/format you used. Check identity, format and page count before submitting.
4. Review **Book Details**. A usable catalog edition locks the book's verified metadata/pages and can be approved automatically. For an audiobook, the score may use an ebook/print page equivalent rather than audio minutes.
5. If there is no usable scoring page count, continue with manual entry and provide a helpful reference link.

A personal Hardcover connection is not required for this shared search. If **Hardcover search is not connected**, you can still use the manual form.

### Fill in reading information

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Title**, **Author** | Correct book details; supplied by a selected catalog book when used. |
| **Book Format** | Paperback, Hardcover, E-book, Audio, Manga, Fanfic or Other. |
| **Started On** | Optional reading start date; cannot be after completion. |
| **Completed On** | Actual finish date, inside this Challenge's date range. |
| **Submitted Pages** | Book pages, excluding bonuses. Must be positive; catalog selections supply locked scoring pages. |
| **Reference Link** | Optional exact-edition or page-count evidence, especially useful for manual entries. |
| **Notes** | Useful information for reviewers. |
| **Theme Claims** | Select applicable Themes, answer their prompts, and follow date/stacking rules. |

Select **Submit Book**. You return to the Challenge. Manual books show **Pending review** until staff decide them. A usable Hardcover selection is **Approved** automatically, though Theme claims can still be pending.

### Manual entry

Select **Clear and Enter Manually**, enter the details above, and **Submit Book**. You do not need to connect Hardcover first. If an old catalog selection expires, select the edition again or use manual entry.

### Status and mistakes

Check the Challenge submissions table or your available Participant Detail; **My Stats → My Book History** also shows your personal records/status.

- **Pending review:** no approved base pages yet.
- **Approved:** approved base pages count; additional claims/matches may still need review.
- **Rejected:** no approved base pages. Read any available review explanation or ask the Host.

There is no normal Reader edit/delete/resubmit control for an existing entry. Send the Host the Challenge, book and specific error. Do not submit an extra copy as a correction unless the Host has arranged the appropriate handling. Staff can change manual review decisions during review, but do not have a normal title/date editor on that review form. Incorrect catalog-verified entries need staff handling because catalog pages are locked.

## 5. Tracking progress

### Pages and scores

**Approved base pages + bonuses/modifiers = total score.** A 300-page book plus an approved 50-page Theme claim gives 300 Base, 50 Modifier, 350 Total. Enter 300 as the book pages, not 350.

The Challenge table can label these **Submitted**, **Base**, **Bonus**, **Final**, **Status**. Reports use **Base**, **Modifier**, **Total**. Some rewards apply to your whole Challenge score instead of a single book, so adding only submission-row bonuses may not reproduce your total.

Pending/rejected/removed books do not contribute approved base pages. Themes require approved claims; BOTM/TBR need qualifying matches. A missing bonus is not always missing reading credit.

### Personal history

Username menu → **My Stats** shows **Verified Pages**, **Approved Books**, **Groups**, **Months Participated**, **Group History**, **Monthly History** and **My Book History**.

Despite the name, the summary **Verified Pages** includes modifiers. Individual book-history rows show approved base pages, or submitted pages when not approved. Those two displays can differ legitimately.

Select your own name from an available Challenge participant/team list to open Participant Detail. It brings together your available feature progress, submissions and score sources. Some score/modifier sections depend on the Challenge's visibility policy. Other Readers' full details are restricted even when their names or scores are visible.

### Teams and standings

Use **Teams**, team detail and **Team Comparison** when offered. A Host chooses who can see Reader scores and team standings; missing information is not automatically zero. Current team placement affects team totals.

Where reporting filters are available, check **From**, **To**, **Show Bonuses** and **Show Readers**. Use **Apply Filters**; **Reset Filters** returns to the usual view. A filtered **Displayed Team Total** can differ from another unfiltered screen. **Current Readers** is a roster count.

### Goals and checkpoints

Staff may configure checkpoints, such as reaching 25% of a 600-page target: 150 pages. They choose approved base pages or bonus-inclusive Total. A historical average can be used instead of a fixed target; new Readers with no history may not be evaluated against an average.

Checkpoints are staff-facing snapshots, not your own goal-editing screen. Later reading does not rewrite an earlier result. A below-target result does not automatically deduct points or remove you. Ask a Host what target your community is using.

## 6. Challenge types

These activities can be combined in one Challenge. You still submit finished books through **Submit Book**.

### Standard Challenge

Register, read books that meet the community's rules, submit them during Active, and follow approval and scores. Teams and optional bonuses depend on the event. Northbound does not require you to finish a fixed number of books just to remain a Reader.

### Book of the Month (BOTM)

Open **Book of the Month** for the Host-selected list, covers, page counts, progress and advertised rewards. The active list can contain one to nine books.

Read a listed book and submit it normally. A qualifying approved completion can earn its per-book bonus. Completing the entire configured set may earn an extra reward. If there are three selected books, finishing two does not earn the all-three reward. A book's page approval and its BOTM match are distinct checks; ask a Host about pending matches. There is no separate Table/Posters toggle or special BOTM submission form.

### Personal TBR

TBR means your planned “to be read” list for this Challenge. It is optional when enabled.

1. While registering, in **Personal TBR**, use **Search Hardcover** with your own personal connection, or **Enter manually**.
2. Manual books need **Title** and **Author**; **Page Count (Optional)** and **Cover URL (Optional)** can be blank.
3. Add up to nine books using **Add Book**. Organize with **Move Up**, **Move Down**, **Remove**.
4. On Registration Summary, check the list and bonus warnings; **Back to Edit** before confirmation if necessary.
5. **Confirm Registration locks the list**, including an empty one.
6. Later open **Personal TBR** for progress and submit finished books normally.

Search results and selected rows show cover thumbnails; missing or unavailable
artwork uses a placeholder. A selected Hardcover edition keeps its artwork when
you save. For a manual book, you may enter an HTTP or HTTPS image URL in
**Cover URL (Optional)**. The URL is validated and your browser loads the preview;
there are no image uploads or server-side image downloads. Existing entries
without cover URLs continue to work.


A confirmed empty list earns no TBR rewards. Fewer than nine books can still earn each qualifying per-book reward; completing a shorter list does not earn the **Full 9-Book** bonus. That requires nine registered books and all nine qualifying completions. Answer editing and rejoining do not unlock the list.

Unlike submission/BOTM search, TBR catalog search uses your **personal** connection. You can connect it without enabling library synchronization, or enter your TBR manually.

### Ask staff for TBR assistance

If you need help, Group Owners and Moderators can manage Challenge TBRs in their
Group. Hosts and Floaters can help within their assigned Challenge; Team Leaders
can help only Readers currently on their own team. They use **Manage TBR** to add,
remove, replace or reorder selections, including protected corrections after your
normal editing lock. Books with match or award history must keep their selection
and position; edits that conflict with that history are blocked.

Staff can **Save TBR** without finalizing it, or **Save & Complete TBR** when all
nine selections are valid. Completing the TBR does not complete Challenge
registration, answer required questions or mean you have read the books. Submit
finished books normally. Your usual self-service editing lock remains in place.

You do not need to connect Hardcover for staff assistance. Staff search uses their
own authorized connection, then the Group catalog connection, or manual entry.
They never use your private Hardcover credentials. TBR changes never synchronize
reading activity to staff or Group accounts; your own optional, consented,
forward-only sync stays separate.

### Themes and Games


New Theme rewards are percentages of **Approved Pages** (not submitted pages or
bonus-inclusive totals). For example, 400 approved pages at 25% earns 100 extra
pages. Each stacked Theme is rounded separately, with half pages rounded up, and
bonuses do not compound. Existing legacy Themes still award fixed pages. A zero
bonus can still be an approved completion. If staff intentionally correct Approved
Pages, percentage rewards change with that correction; ordinary repeat review does
not change an established award. Ask a Host about corrections.

**Themes** are optional prompts with dates and bonus pages. Select **Theme Claims** during submission and answer any attached prompt. Completed On must fit the Theme window. A Theme that disallows stacking cannot be combined with another Theme on the same book. Staff approve claims separately from pages.

**Games** are optional Challenge activities. A Host awards their bonus pages to individuals, teams or eligible Challenge participants. Those are modifiers, not extra books/base pages. If staff void a reward its score effect is removed; ask the Host to explain a correction.

## 7. Hardcover

Hardcover is an external book catalog and personal-library service. Connecting is optional; manual book entry remains available.

### Connect your account

1. My Account → **Personal Hardcover Connection**.
2. Select **Connect with Hardcover** when available.
3. Review and approve the provider request, return to Northbound, and check **Connected** and permissions.
4. Existing connections may show **Reconnect / Update Permissions** or **Switch to OAuth**.

If OAuth is unavailable, **Advanced: scoped API key** accepts your **Personal Hardcover API Token** → **Connect with Scoped API Key**. It should have `read:catalog`, `read:library`, `write:library`. Keep it private. **Test Saved Connection** can check access, though an API-key catalog test does not prove every library-write permission. Use the displayed replacement/reconnect control for expired access.

The personal connection supports your TBR lookup and optional library sync. Your Group's separate connection supports shared submissions/BOTM lookup; it cannot authorize changes to your personal library.

### Choose synchronization yourself

Under **Hardcover Synchronization**, both preferences start OFF:

- **Sync completed books:** eligible new approvals can mark a safely matched book Read in your personal Hardcover library.
- **Sync Northbound completion dates:** also sends the actual finish date as a read occurrence; requires Sync completed books.

Choose the options you want → **Save Synchronization Preferences**. Connecting alone does not turn either on. Enable consent **before** the new approvals you want considered for sync.

Northbound uses book/library information needed for lookup and safe matching. It can create a missing library entry or update its Read status and, with date consent, the related finish-date occurrence. It does not import your Hardcover reading history, export old Northbound approvals, or write ratings/reviews/catalog edits/deletions. Manual/unmatched books are not guaranteed automatic export. Distinct rereads may have separate date occurrences.

Sync runs in the background. Provider problems do not invalidate your Northbound approval/score. **Sync Now** wakes eligible work already waiting; it does not export historical books. My Account health may show **Pending**, **Retry scheduled**, **Reconnect required**, **Reconciliation required**, or **Recovery review**. Follow reconnect instructions when requested; ask staff about persistent review holds rather than repeatedly clicking Sync Now.

### Disconnect

**Personal Hardcover Connection → Disconnect** acts immediately without a second confirmation screen. It clears the saved connection and switches sync OFF. Your Northbound reading history and TBR remain, as does reading already sent to Hardcover. If provider revocation fails, remove authorization in Hardcover too.

Reconnecting does not automatically switch consent back on or export old history. Existing eligible unfinished work may resume when connection and consent allow it.

## 8. Frequently asked questions

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Do I need Hardcover? | No. You can register, plan TBR manually and submit books manually. Shared search can work without your own connection. |
| Can I change a submission? | There is no normal Reader edit/delete control. Contact a Host with the exact mistake. |
| What if I chose the wrong book or was rejected? | Ask the Host about correction/removal/recovery. Do not submit duplicates to bypass a rejection. |
| How are pages counted? | Approved non-removed book pages form Base; eligible rewards form Modifier; Total is their sum. Catalog pages are locked when automatically verified. |
| Do audiobook minutes become pages? | A catalog audiobook can use an ebook/print page equivalent. If unavailable, enter manually with evidence for staff; there is no promised minutes-to-pages formula. |
| Can I participate without a team? | Yes, active enrollment is enough for submission. Ask a Host about the community's placement policy. |
| Can I join multiple Challenges? | Yes, participation is separate for each eligible Challenge/Group. Check each one's dates/rules. There is no single-Challenge-per-account limit. |
| What happens when the Challenge ends? | In Finalizing new submissions stop while staff finish review. Completed/Archived retains history. Registration can close earlier without ending submissions. |
| Why can't I register or submit? | Registration needs Upcoming/Active plus Open registration; submission needs Active plus active participation. Staff removal or Floater staffing can also prevent it. |
| Why is my bonus missing? | Check book approval, claim/match status and list requirements. Short TBR lists never earn the full nine-book reward. |
| Why can't I see scores? | Hosts choose score/standings visibility. This does not necessarily mean your books failed to count. |
| Can I change my TBR later? | No normal edit after Confirm Registration, even if answers remain editable. Check it before confirming. |
| Will Sync Now send all my books? | No. Only existing eligible queued work is processed; there is no history backfill. |

## 9. Quick start

**Create Account → Join Group → Register for Challenge → Read Book → Submit Book → Track Progress**

- [ ] Sign in and check My Account.
- [ ] Join with the community's code.
- [ ] Read the Challenge dates and rules.
- [ ] Add any optional TBR **before** Confirm Registration.
- [ ] Check team placement; it is separate from joining.
- [ ] Submit completed books while the Challenge is Active.
- [ ] Add applicable Theme claims and prompt answers.
- [ ] Follow book approval, feature progress and available standings.
- [ ] Ask a Host about mistakes rather than entering a duplicate.
- [ ] If desired, connect Hardcover and enable consent before future approvals.
